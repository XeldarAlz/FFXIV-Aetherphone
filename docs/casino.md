# Gamba

Gamba is the casino app (app id `casino`, route prefix `/casino`, key prefix `casino.`). Coins become chips at the cashier, and those chips play every house game on one floor-wide bankroll. Hosted tables can also run on practice chips or, for venues, on gil banked by the host. This page describes the client as it ships: the economy it mirrors, the stores, the stage kit every play screen runs on, the cabinets and their playback, hosting and the venue layer, the lobby, and how to add a game. The HTTP routes and realtime room kinds are listed in [Networking](networking.md#casino-routes).

The server decides and the client performs. Every outcome is drawn by the server from a committed seed; the client only choreographs a result it has already been sent (reels decelerate onto it, the ball bounces along its path, the race script ends in its order) and never fabricates a near miss.

## Economy on the client

The client mirrors the economy for display only; every amount that moves money is checked by the server again.

- **Rate.** `CasinoChipLots.ChipPerCoin` is 1,000 chips per coin, both ways. Chips stay a currency of their own; every chip balance also prints its coin value (`ChipValue.Coins`, cached by `ChipValueText`).
- **Buying chips.** `POST /casino/chips/buy {coins, clientActionId}` opens a bankroll or tops up the live one in one idempotent call (`CasinoStore.BuyChips`), from 1 coin up to the whole wallet; a short wallet answers `insufficient`. The answer carries the new stack, the wallet balance and the ceiling, which the store absorbs before the next frame.
- **Cashing out.** `POST /casino/sittings/close` converts the whole bankroll in one call, whole coins, with the sub-coin residue kept by the house. There is no daily cash-out limit and no buy-in limit; a blackjack rack keeps its own table band (`CasinoHostingRules.ChipMinBuyIn` to `ChipMaxBuyIn`, or the table's `minBuyIn` and `maxBuyIn`).
- **Max win per bet.** `GET /casino` sends `maxWinPerBet` (chips, policy data, 50,000 coins by default). The server clamps the net win of every house-banked round to it and marks the round `capped`; Hold'em pots and the floor jackpot never meet it. The info sheet and the pay tables print it through `MaxWinText`, and a capped result passes `Capped` in its `CasinoBetRecord`, which shows "Max win per bet reached" on the stage.
- **Ladder and ceiling.** `CasinoLadder` mirrors the global ladder (27 rungs from 100 to 50B), `LevelCap(level)` (geometric between the anchors, level 1 at 10K up to level 100 at 50B) and `MaxBet(level, balance, anchors, maxWin) = min(max(LevelCap, balance / 20), maxWin / 10)` floored to a rung (`MaxWinBetMultiple`), so every win up to 10x at the ceiling pays in full and the max win clamp only meets the long tail. The ceiling's `reason` is `level`, `balance` or `max_win`. `CasinoStore.Ceiling` returns the server's `ceiling` block when present and falls back to the local formula against an older floor. Every player-chosen stake snaps to the ladder; the server refuses the rest with `ladder` or `ceiling`.
- **Bonuses.** `CasinoBonuses` claim `welcome`, `timed`, `reload`, `streak`, `levelup`, `broke` and `rebate` through `POST /casino/bonus/{kind}/claim`, from the Floor hero and the missions sheet. Grants land as chips in the bankroll (opening one when none is live).
- **Levels, club, titles.** `GET /casino` carries `progress` (level, XP bounds, lifetime figures, balance title) and `club` (tier, points, multiplier, rebate rate). `LevelCapsule`, `StatusTitle` and the club capsule and sheet render them. The Floor tier card and the home widget take the tier from the level (`StatusTitle.ForLevel`: Shark 20, High Roller 40, VIP 60, Whale 80, Legend 100), so cashing out or buying chips never moves it; the seat badges on the felts still follow the table stack (`StatusTitle.For`: Shark 1M, High Roller 10M, VIP 100M, Whale 1B, Legend 1T).
- **Feature flags.** `GET /casino` sends `features[]`, parsed once into a `CasinoFeatureSet` (`CasinoFeatures` holds the names: `economy.v3`, `bonus`, `levels`, `club`, `hosting.v2`, `venue`, `gil.tables`, `roomcodes`, `machines`, `missions`, `challenges`, `fame`, `feed`, `rain`, `holdem`, `race`, `plinko`, `originals`, `venue.book`). `CasinoGameGate.FlagFor(gameId)` maps a game to its flag, and a game whose flag is missing shows a "Not open yet" tile instead of opening, so a new client degrades cleanly against an older server.
- **Limits.** The self-set daily loss limit stays opt-in (0 on the wire means no limit); see the gotcha in [Networking](networking.md#gotchas).

## Stores

Every store follows the house pattern: requests run off the draw thread through `StoreWork`, results are parked in a field and the draw path takes them once with a `Take*` method (`TakeSpinResult`, `TakeSeatOutcome`, `TakeFailure`), so Draw never awaits and never allocates for a result. Built in `PhoneServices`.

| Store | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| `CasinoStore` | `GET /casino` (state, sittings, features, ceiling, max win, bonuses, club, progress), one-call chip buys, cash-out, limits, bonus claims, the per-character Auto top-up switch |
| `CasinoPlayStore` | Solo machine routes: slot spins, the Golden Bird gamble, Moogle Money meters, scratch, Barkeep, and the pending-round recovery (`PendingCasinoRound`, per character); owns `Originals` (`CasinoOriginalsStore`) and `Plinko` (`CasinoPlinkoStore`) |
| `CasinoRoomsStore` | The room list, the one live `CasinoRoomSession` (attach, snapshot, events with seq and epoch, private lanes, clock skew), wheel and race bets and bingo cards |
| `CasinoTablesStore` | The table directory, quick seat, blackjack seats, hosting (create, door, invites, kick, rename, co-dealers, pause, deal, tournament, close), the per-table ledger cache with its record, settle and remove actions, nearby tables |
| `HoldemStore` | Hold'em seats and actions, time bank, sit out, top-up, hand history, the rejoin penalty |
| `CasinoVenueStore` | Venue room GameState (`VenueRoomView`), venue acts, roll and raffle verification, tournaments, trade-sync proposals |
| `CasinoFloorStore` | Missions and claims, challenges, Hall of Fame boards, the bets feed, the `casino-floor` watch with its ticker and rain notes |
| `CasinoHistoryStore` | History pages, round verification, and hosted table hands recorded locally (practice and gil hands never reach the server's history) |
| `CasinoSpinStore` | The free daily spin, which pays wallet coins rather than chips |

`CasinoRoomSession` parses the snapshot's GameState into one typed slot per kind (`Wheel`, `Bingo`, `Blackjack`, `Race`, `Holdem`); venue GameState is parsed by `CasinoVenueStore`. A sequence gap or an epoch change triggers a resync. A `restarting` or `draining` answer (a deploy) or an attach `cooldown` keeps the room and its last state instead of closing it: the session shows the reconnect veil, attaches again on the next connection and retries on its own every four seconds while the socket stays up. A room read that answers not found asks the table card why before it closes the room, so a player outside the door sees Ask to join (or the waiting, denied or removed card) rather than an ended table. `CasinoSeatMachine` and `CasinoJoinGate` hold the seat states and the "joins next hand" rules shared by the tables, and `CasinoTurnNotifier` posts a notification when your turn comes while the table is not in view.

## Stage frame

Every play screen draws inside one host frame, `CasinoStage`. The lobby, cashier, history, fairness and limits pages keep the normal app chrome; cabinets, tables and rooms go full bleed with no `AppHeader`.

The host (`CasinoApp.DrawStage`) opens `AppSurface.BeginEdgeToEdge(content, true)` and then, per frame:

```csharp
var frame = stage.Begin(area, spec, balance, casino.Ceiling);
cabinet.Draw(stage, frame, ui);
var action = stage.End(ui);
```

`Begin` draws the backdrop and the frosted deck, advances effects, and returns a `CasinoStageFrame`: `Layout`, `Safe`, `Deck`, `Body`, `Full`, `DeltaSeconds`, `Phase` (the lights clock), `Instant`, `Focused` and `SnapToTruth`. `End` draws particles, the celebration, screen effects, the practice ribbon, the bets handle and the chrome, and returns `CasinoStageAction.Back` or `Cashier` for the host to act on. Overlays draw at window level through `stage.Gate()` and `stage.DrawOverlays(screen, ui)`; `TakeInfoRequest()` returns `Rules`, `Extra` or `Fairness`, and `TakeRoundRequest()` a round id tapped in the bets rail.

`CasinoStageSpec(GameId, Title, Preset, Room, DeckHeight, Practice, BetsRail, InstantAvailable, ReturnTenths, Extra, Warmth, LampPool)` declares the screen: `Room` adds the 40 unit phase ribbon, `DeckHeight` reserves the bet deck (0 for layouts that draw their own controls), `Practice` swaps the capsule to grey Practice chips and adds the practice ribbon, `ReturnTenths` prints the return in the info sheet, `Extra` names a second info button (a pay table, odds sheet or the table sheet), `History` names a recent hands button (blackjack), `Warmth` tints the Strip toward amber, `LampPool` lights the felt. `CasinoApp.StageSpecFor(route)` picks the spec per screen.

Geometry, in design units times `UiScale.Current`: chrome band 52; back chip and info chip 36 glass circles centred 28 in from either side at y 26; the chips capsule centred on the band; ribbon 40 and practice ribbon 22 under the band; deck at the bottom across the full screen width (`BetComposer.DeckHeightFor(knob, fixedAmount)` returns exactly its rows); `Safe` sits between them, inset 12. Worlds fit inside `Safe`; lights and backdrops use `Full`. `CasinoStageLayout.ChromeContains(point)` tells a cabinet whether a press landed on the chrome.

The band holds the back chip, the chips capsule and the info chip and nothing else. The capsule is fitted in `Begin` (`ChromeCapsule.Fit`, exposed as `stage.Capsule`): it drops the full coin value, then the compact one, before it would outgrow the room between the two chips. Room status (timers, crowd, history strips) goes under the band, in the ribbon a spec reserves with `Room: true` (`Layout.Ribbon`). In landscape (`Layout.Landscape`) the band is the back chip alone. Short notices such as "Max win per bet reached" sit just above the deck through `Layout.Notice(width, height, scale)`, never over a room's status line.

Snap to truth: when the stage was not drawn for more than 2 seconds, or the phone regains focus after more than 2 seconds away, `frame.SnapToTruth` is true for one frame, the balance and the celebration snap to their final state, and cabinets finish their own animations.

`stage.RepeatPressed()` reports Space while the stage has focus, no overlay is open and no text field is active, claiming only the Space key through `GameInput`.

## Full screen and legibility

Binding for every cabinet, table and room:

1. The game is the screen. The world (felt, track, reels, board, wheel) fills the stage edge to edge under the glass chrome. No boxed play area, no inset panel, no `GameScene.Arena`. Felt tables are the backdrop itself, with seats sitting on it.
2. Night, never bright. Backdrops stay at or under 0.22 luminance; no bright saturated full-screen fills. Text sits on a dark backdrop or on glass.
3. Readable text. Body copy at least `Subheadline`, status lines at least `Footnote` in strong ink (`StageText.Strong`), amounts at least `Title3`, the primary state line ("Place your bets", "Your turn") at least `Title2` with a soft shadow. Muted ink only on glass or dark felt and only for secondary labels. Contrast at least 4.5:1 against the brightest point behind the text.
4. One obvious next action: one full-width primary in the bet deck, secondary actions as smaller pills beside it. Empty seats are large Sit spots, not small "Open" circles.
5. Use the space: worlds grow to fill Safe, nothing smaller than a 44 unit touch target.
6. Clean entry: one tap from the Floor into any game, Back returns to where you came from, rules one tap away on the info chip.
7. Every idle state has motion and every state change has feedback.

### Stage text

`StageText` is the one way to put words on the world. Each helper resolves the requested style up to its role minimum (`StageText.Minimum(role)`, never shrinking below it) and either fits or marquees against its container:

- `State(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id)`: Title2 or larger in strong ink with a soft shadow; overflow moves onto a glass capsule and marquees. `StateLine(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, ink)` is the id-free form that ellipsizes.
- `Status(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id, overWorld)`: Footnote or larger in strong ink, on a dark glass capsule when it sits over the world. `Status(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, scale)` and `Plate(..., ink, style, scale)` are the id-free capsule forms.
- `Amount(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id)`: Title3 or larger in gold.
- `Label(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id, muted)`: Footnote or larger; a muted label always sits on the capsule.
- `FitScale(text, maxWidth, style, role)`: the shrink-to-fit scale clamped at the role minimum.

`StageText.CapsuleFill` is dark enough that muted ink reads at 4.5:1 even over the brightest felt; `StageContrast.Ratio` and `Over` are the WCAG math the tests use.

### Felt tables, seats and the action row

`FeltTable.DrawCloth(drawList, full, rail, practice, scale)` paints the night cloth over any rect (a grey cloth for practice tables) for scenes that are not on a Felt stage, such as idle previews and Hold'em's own felt. A `FeltTable` instance draws the table layer over a Felt stage with `Draw(drawList, full, table, new FeltTableOptions(seats, print, cloth, rail, circles, practice), scale)`: a gold insurance arc, betting circles and an optional printed line such as "BLACKJACK PAYS 3 TO 2" set along an arc in low-contrast gold, and returns `FeltTableGeometry` (dealer anchor at the top centre, the seat arc, `Seat(index)`, `BettingCircle(index)`), all scaled to the table rect with seats of at least 28 units radius.

`SeatSpot.DrawEmpty(drawList, center, radius, label, accent, invite, scale)` is the empty seat: a dark disc of at least 56 units across with a plus glyph and a "Sit" label, pulsing a glow while `invite` is set (you are not seated), returning true on a tap.

`DeckActions.Row(deck, scale)` is the bottom row of the bet deck, 56 units tall and full width; every other deck row is 44 (`DeckActions.SecondaryHeight`) with 8 between rows and 12 of side padding. `stage.SecondaryAction(label, enabled, ink)` lays out up to two pills from the left and `stage.PrimaryAction(label, enabled, ink)` takes the rest, the whole row when there are none. Pills are as wide as their measured label plus 14 a side (`DeckActions.PillWidth`, 44 minimum) and are never cut: the primary gives way from 140 down to an 88 floor first. A row with more pills than it can hold goes on one pannable rail through `DeckActions.Rail(chipRail, row, ui, labels, active)`; `DeckActions.DrawPill` and `DrawIconPill` draw single content-sized pills. `DeckActions.Above` places a status line over the row, and `DeckActions.Slice` splits it for two equal choices such as Insure and No insurance.

## Lights, signs and colours

`CasinoLights` is static and allocation free: `BulbChase(drawList, rect, radius, scale, phase, spacing, colorA, colorB, lit)` (6 unit bulbs on a 14 unit pitch, every third dark, chasing at 6 bulbs a second), `Spotlight(drawList, origin, direction, length, spread, color, alpha)`, `Bokeh(drawList, rect, phase, density, scale)`, `CoinShower(scale)` and `CoinShowerEmitter(scale, rate)` (gold discs, gravity 180 units, a pulse size curve), `Sparkle`, `Shard`, `Ring` particle specs, and `LightSweep(backdrop, strength)`. `CasinoSigns.Draw(drawList, CasinoSign, center, height, color, lit)` strokes a sign in neon (`HeightToFit` sizes it to a box); a new game adds its word to the `CasinoSign` enum and the word table. The sign font is proportional: each letter is a set of tube paths on a 6 unit cap height, `r` corners bend on a 1.6 unit radius, and letters sit a fixed gap apart measured from their ink (plus optional optical bearings for open shapes like T, L, A and V). `NeonTubeMesh` builds every tube as one mesh with round joins and caps and a feathered glow, so no letter grows spikes or sits higher or lower than its neighbors. `CasinoColors` holds `Money`, `MoneyHighlight`, `LightA` (rose), `LightB` (cyan), the felt pair, the warm inks, `Practice` and `Loss`. `AppPalettes.Gamba` is the app palette; the casino accent is `AccentRing.Rose`.

## Win celebrations

`stage.Celebration.Celebrate(stake, payout, origin, instant, jackpot)` picks a tier from the net win over the stake (`WinLadder.TierFor`): Win above 0, Nice from 3x, Big from 10x, Mega from 25x, Epic from 50x, Legendary from 100x or any jackpot. A payout at or below the stake returns `WinTier.None` and does nothing: no light, no particles, no sound, the paying symbols simply light. Each tier carries its sparkles or confetti, coin shower seconds, light sweep, banner, bulb chase, full-screen card, sound and count-up seconds (`WinLadder.Spec`). Big and up flash, punch, vignette or slow the game. A tap on Safe skips after half a second; Instant mode shows the final amount with a 0.3 second pop. `Blocking` is true while an Epic or Legendary card covers the stage. `WinCelebration` is the only celebration path in the app.

`CasinoSfx.Play(sound)`, `Pitched(sound, step)` and `Win(spec)` play only while the stage is drawn and focused, and WinBig, WinEpic and Fanfare share one 10 second slot (a throttled big win falls back to WinSmall). The casino cues (`UiSound.ReelTick` through `LevelUp`) are synthesized by tools/sound-generator (`python generate-sounds.py --casino`).

## Bet composer and the ceiling

`BetComposer.Draw(stage, ui, frame.Deck, model, delta)` returns `Confirm`, `StartAuto`, `StopAuto` or `None`. `BetComposerModel(MinimumBet, MaximumBet, Stack, Action, Enabled, AutoAvailable, FixedAmount, Knob, Repeat, Busy, Cost, Rack)`: `MaximumBet` is the game's own cap clamped to `casino.Ceiling.MaxBet`; `Cost` is what the action takes from the stack when that differs from the amount (an ante, a bonus buy, a Dealer Hold'em deal); `Rack` marks a table rack, the one stack the composer clamps to; `Action` is a one-argument template ("Buy {0}", "Bet {0}") that receives the compact amount; `Knob` reserves `composer.KnobRect` (a 44 unit row) for the game's own control (risk, rows, mines, target, runner, spot, card count); `FixedAmount` hides the amount row. The amount row (`DeckAmountRow`) is the field plus the quick chips "½", "2×" and the localized Max, each sized to its label; the field keeps an 88 unit floor and "½" then "2×" step aside on rows too narrow for all three. The field taps into free input and commits on Enter or blur; every value snaps to the ladder through `CasinoLadder.Clamp`, `Half`, `Double` and `Top`. With `AutoAvailable` the action row starts with a content-sized Auto pill (repeat icon and label, folding to the icon on a narrow row) that opens the auto settings sheet (`composer.Gate()` and `composer.DrawOverlay(screen, ui, bonusAvailable)`); the primary is always the manual action. The sheet's own "Start auto" button starts the run on the next frame (`AutoBetPlan.RouteStart` starts a covered run, tops the stack up when auto top-up can, or opens Get chips) and returns `StartAuto`; while it runs the primary reads "Stop (12 left)" and returns `StopAuto`. `composer.AutoArmed` stays true from opening the sheet until the next manual bet (Mines plans its auto picks then). `composer.Auto` is the `AutoBetPlan`: the cabinet starts the next round with `Auto.Next` while `Auto.Running`, and reports each settled round with `Auto.Settle(stake, payout, bonus, min, max, stack)`, which applies on-win and on-loss adjustments and stops on count, profit, loss, bonus or chips. The info sheet prints the ceiling, its reason and the max win per bet.

**Get chips.** A bankroll bet is not clamped to the stack: when its cost is above the stack (or there is no bankroll), the primary action becomes Get chips through `ChipsDesk.Primary`, which opens `GetChipsSheet` over the stage. The sheet shows the wallet, the chips with their coin value, three amounts sized to the bet (`ChipsAmounts.ForNeed`: enough for 10, 50 and 100 bets, or 1, 2 and 5 buy-ins at a table, rounded up to whole coins and clamped to the wallet), a custom amount and the Auto top-up switch (`Configuration.CasinoAutoTopUp`, per character, off by default). With Auto top-up on, `AutoTopUpRule` buys enough for ten bets when the wallet covers the bet, and the composer places the bet once the chips land; auto play asks `composer.Covers(stage, need, stack)` before each round. A refused buy rests auto top-up for 30 seconds. Every deck that is not a composer (the race deck, the blackjack and Hold'em sit decks) calls `ChipsDesk.DrawGetChips` for the same action, so the cashier is never the only way in. Every buy toasts "Bought {chips} chips for {coins} coins", or notes it in the cashier when that is open.

Gil is not on the ladder, so gil blackjack tables bet through `ClassicBetComposer`: the same amount row (a free gil field with ½, 2× and Max) over a 56 unit primary, bounded by the host's limits and the seat's stack. The race deck (`RaceDeck` over `RaceDeckLayout`) shares the amount row under its bet kind switch, labels its button "Bet 100" and shows the pick ("Saffron Dash · 5.62x", from `RaceDeck.Caption`) or the prompt as a fitted caption just above it. Hold'em raises use `HoldemRaiseComposer` (Min, half pot, three quarters, Pot and All in as 44 unit chips on a pannable rail, a slider and a one big blind stepper, the button label always the exact amount).

Every settled chip round goes through `stage.Settle(new CasinoBetRecord(game, stake, payout, roundId, settledAtUnixMs, capped))`, which feeds the bets rail's My bets tab. The bets rail's All bets and High rollers tabs read `GET /casino/feed`.

## Playback

A playback class turns a server answer into choreography and is the only thing a cabinet animates from. The shape is the same everywhere: `Begin` or `Update` takes the DTO (or the room state plus the server clock), `Advance` moves time, the cabinet drains cues with `TryTake*` or `Take*` (a tick, a landing, a reveal, a settle) to fire sounds, particles and the celebration, and `Snap` or a snap flag jumps to the final state for a mid-event join, a reconnect or `frame.SnapToTruth`. Playbacks hold no ImGui state, so the tests drive them directly (`*PlaybackTests`). Client-side flourishes that need randomness (a ball's wobble, a patron's walk, commentary lines) draw from `GameRandom` seeded from the round, so the same round replays identically.

| Playback | Replays |
| --- | --- |
| `MachineRoundPlayback`, `MachineRollup` | A spin's `steps[]` beat by beat (spin, tumble, expand, hold, respin, collect, meter), then the rollup at half the bet a second up to 20x |
| `PlinkoFlight` | Each ball along its server `path`, a scripted bounce per row, up to ten balls in flight |
| `DiceRollPlayback`, `LimboClimbPlayback`, `KenoDrawPlayback` | The instant originals: the sliding roll marker, the climbing multiplier, drawn tiles in sequence (Mines and Hi-Lo keep their open round in `MinesBoard` and `HiLoChain`) |
| `ScratchCardPlayback` | The foil reveal of a bought ticket |
| `WheelRoundPlayback`, `WheelChoreography`, `WheelPointer` | The wheel landing on the server's segment |
| `BingoRoundPlayback` | Called balls, flights, daubs and stage wins (a burst of balls after a join jumps straight to the board) |
| `RaceRoundPlayback` | The race from `RaceScript`, rebuilt from the order and seed revealed at Locked |
| `BlackjackDealPlayback`, `BlackjackDealChoreography` | Card flights, the hole card reveal and per-seat settlement |
| `HoldemPlayback` | Hole cards, board streets, reveals at showdown and chips sweeping to winners |
| `DailySpinPlayback` | The sprung pointer of the free spin |
| `DiceTablePlayback`, `DeathrollPlayback`, `RafflePlayback`, `TournamentPlayback` | Venue rolls, duels, the raffle draw and the blackjack tournament strip |

## Rules mirrors, vectors and fairness

Core/Casino holds one Dalamud-free mirror per game (`BlackjackRules`, `BlackjackSideBets`, `HoldemRules`, `HoldemHands`, `RaceRules`, `RaceScript`, `PlinkoRules`, `OriginalsRules`, `SlotsRules`, `SlotsMachines`, `GoldenBirdRules`, `CrystalCascadeRules`, `MoogleMoneyRules`, `ScratchRules`, `WheelRules`, `BingoRules`, `BarkeepRules`, `DailySpinRules`, `VenueRules`, `CasinoHostingRules`, `CasinoFloorRules`, `CasinoLadder`). They exist to print pay tables, returns, bands and odds, and to replay draws for verification; they never decide a payout. When a backend constant changes, the matching mirror and its `*RulesTests` change in the same release.

Seeded games share vector files with the backend: `src/Aetherphone.Tests/Vectors/` holds `holdem.json`, `race.json`, `plinko.json`, `originals.json`, `slots-bird.json`, `slots-cascade.json`, `slots-moogle.json`, `blackjack-sidebets.json` and `venue.json`, each a byte-for-byte copy of the file in the backend's test project. Every vector test first pins the file's SHA-256, so a drifted copy fails loudly, then replays each case through the client mirror (`HoldemVectorTests`, `RaceVectors`, `PlinkoVectorTests`, `OriginalsVectorTests`, `SlotsVectorTests`, `BlackjackSideBetVectorTests`, `CasinoVenueVectorTests`). Never edit a vector by hand; copy the backend's file again.

`CasinoVerifier.Verify` checks a settled round from `GET /casino/rounds/{roundId}/verify`: the revealed seed must hash to the commit, then the draw log must replay purpose by purpose (`segment`, `card`, `ball`, `shuffle`, `prize`, `patrons`, `jackpot`, `gamble`, `peg`, `field`, `strength`, `runner`, and the originals' float stream with `mine`, `roll`, `limbo`, `keno`, `card`). Hold'em hands and blackjack hands verify through the room's hand index (`GET /casino/rooms/{roomId}/verify/{index}`), and venue rolls and raffle draws through `VenueVerifier` over `VenueDraws`. Verification fails closed: anything it cannot replay is a mismatch, never a pass.

## Tables, hosting, free play and gil

House tables are furniture the server keeps open: four blackjack tables (`blackjack-pit`, `blackjack-parlour`, `blackjack-salon`, `blackjack-vault`) and the Hold'em rooms (`holdem-low`, `holdem-mid`, `holdem-high`, and `holdem-royal` for the Whale title). The four blackjack tables are stake levels, one per tier: the Pit takes 500 to 10K a hand, the Parlour 5K to 100K, the Salon 50K to 1M and the Vault 500K to 10M, all on the standard rule sheet (blackjack pays 3:2, the dealer stands on soft 17, six decks). Every list still shows them as one Blackjack row (`HouseBlackjack.Of` sums the floor, spans the lowest minimum to the highest maximum and picks the busiest table with a seat left). On Live that row's Pick opens a sheet listing the four tables by name, so a player can sit where their friends are, with Find me a seat on top; the Blackjack pit lists the same four tables under its Find me a seat card, both drawn by `HouseRoomCard` over `BlackjackRoomRows`. Each row states its band, and a table whose minimum is above the player's max bet is dimmed and offers Watch only; the server refuses that sit (`ceiling`) and quick seat skips it. Either way, a player who already holds a house seat goes straight back to it. Hold'em rooms do differ by stakes, so each is listed and named by its blinds. Sitting at a chip table moves a rack from the bankroll chip-to-chip; leaving walks it home. One card-table seat per identity across blackjack and Hold'em.

`HostSheet` is one screen that fills a `HostDraft` and builds the `CasinoTableConfig` for `POST /casino/tables`. A game header card, washed with the game's tint, shows its `CasinoArt.GameTile`, name and line, and its Change pill opens a 3 x 2 grid of game chips (`HostGames`: Blackjack, Hold'em, Dice table, Deathroll, Raffle, and Book while the server lists `venue.book`). Play for is three choice tiles, Chips, Free play and Gil: a tile the game refuses is faded and tapping it toasts why (`HostRules.Refusal`), and one hint line describes the chosen currency. The table shows only the numbers that matter, as `LadderSlider` cards over `HostLadders` (a blackjack table's bank on gil and its bets, Hold'em blinds, the dice table's top roll, the deathroll start and stake, a Book's bank, and an info card for a raffle), plus a row of seat discs for blackjack and Hold'em. Who can join is three icon tiles, Anyone, Knock and Invite only, with a hint, and the name is optional. A Table rules row carries a one-line summary of the rules (`HostRules.Summary`, cached on `HostRulesKey`) and pushes `CasinoScreen.HostRules`, where `HostRulesPage` edits the same draft in grouped sections that show only when they apply (`HostRules.Sections`): Dealing, Turns (turn clock, time bank), Blackjack rules (pays, decks, splits, doubles, soft 17, five card Charlie, peek; free play and gil tables only), Hold'em (ante as a share of the big blind), Dice (round length, highest roll wins, and the stake per round on gil), Free play (starting stack, plus rebuys and cards face up at seat games), Gil (largest single payout, blackjack and Book) and Room (spectators at seat games, and Pin to this venue). Back or Done returns to the sheet with every value kept. The pin row (`VenueHostOptions`) names the place it pins to and explains the two cases that would otherwise hide a pinned table in silence (`VenuePinNotes`, computed by the testable `NotesFor`): with Who can join on Invite only the table is listed nowhere, since the nearby query and the Venues pill both skip private tables, so the Room section and the sticky footer say to pick Knock or Anyone (worded for a Book as patrons being unable to find or open it), and the listing itself is never changed; and with the host standing off the plot (a ward but no plot in the read position) the Room section says to stand on the venue's plot so the Venues app can show the table, while Live still lists it for the ward. Dealing offers the dealer seat on free play and gil tables, with an auto deal switch that starts off: off, every hand waits for the host's or a co-dealer's Deal and players see "Bets are open. {0} deals when ready."; on, the betting clock deals as at a house table. On a chip table the section only says the house clock deals, because the server refuses host dealing, co-dealers and pause there (`practice_only`). A Book (`HostGame.Book`, wire kind `casino.book`) is gil only, so its Play for tiles refuse chips and free play, the table asks only for a bank, who can join starts on Anyone (`HostDraft.DefaultListing`, since patrons register on a book by opening it), and its rules page holds only Gil and Room; `HostDraft.BuildBook` sends seats 0, no dealer, no spectators and no rules, and `CasinoBookRules.Check` mirrors the server's check (`gil_only`, `config_invalid`). A sticky glass footer summarises the table above Open the ledger for a Book or Open table for a game, and a refused open shows its reason above the summary. `CasinoHostingRules.Check` names the common conflicts instead of the generic refusal: a minimum bet above the maximum (`min_bet_above_max`), a maximum bet above the practice stack (`max_bet_above_stack`) or the bank (`max_bet_above_bank`), and a max payout below the maximum bet (`payout_below_max_bet`), each message saying which setting to move. `HostFlowLayout` holds the geometry and is pinned by layout tests. The sheet opens from the Host tab (a game poster through `HostSheet.EnterGame`, with the game preselected and gil picked when the game takes gil and gil tables are open, or Host again on the remembered draft) and from the Blackjack and Hold'em pits; every entry goes through `CasinoApp.PushHostSheet`, which hands the sheet its Table rules route. Opening a table successfully remembers the draft for that character (`CasinoPreferences.RememberHostDraft` into `Configuration.CasinoHostDrafts`, a `CasinoHostDraftMemory` of plain values and ladder indexes with a `Version` that discards the draft when the ladders change), and Host again restores every value and rule (`HostSheet.EnterDraft`); a remembered Book draft stays out of sight while the server does not list `venue.book` (`HostDraft.Reopens`).

Hosted tables and venue rooms carry a six-letter room code (`CasinoRoomCodes` mirrors the server's alphabet and normalisation; `joinCode` is only sent to members). The host console shows it in big letter tiles with Copy, `RoomCodeCard` shows it in the table info sheet, and Live joins with `POST /casino/tables/join` (`code_unknown` when no open table uses it; an old `[aep.casino.v1:...]` token pasted into the same field still resolves). The Host tab (`CasinoApp.Host`) opens with an intro line, then either the Your table card or, with no table open, the Host again card and the poster grid, and last a My gil ledger row (`LinkRow`); nearby and listed tables live on Live. The Your table card (`Venue/HostTableCard` over `HostHomeLayout`) shows the game tile, the table name (`TableRowViews.NameOf`: the server stores no default name, so an untitled table or Book reads "{0}'s table" or "{0}'s book" after its host in the reader's language), a line of game, currency and who can join (`TableRowViews.HostLine`), an OPEN or PAUSED pill (`TableStatusPill`), the code in letter tiles (`CodeTiles`) beside a live crowd line that moves under them when it does not fit (read from the door, which the tab keeps fresh, with the knock count, or from the card until the door loads: `HostTableCard.CrowdOf`), Go to table (Open the ledger for a Book) and Manage, and a note that one table runs at a time. Host again (`Venue/HostAgainCard`) shows the remembered draft's game tile and `HostDraftSummary` line with an Open pill, and Pick a game (`Venue/HostGameGrid`) is a 3 x 2 grid of host posters drawn with the `PosterTile` statics, each with its plays-for line and Gil badge, that opens the host sheet on that game. `TableDoor` is the host console: a status card (the game tile, name, line and status pill, the table code caption with six big letter tiles and Copy, the tiles shrinking to leave the pill room in `DoorLayout.CodeRow`, then Go to table in white on rose and Copy invite); Knocking, one gold `KnockCard` per knock with a gentle pulse and Decline and Let in, beside the name when they fit and under it otherwise (`DoorLayout.KnockInline`); Players, the seated and present members merged into one list (`TableDoor.Merge`: seats first, an idle host left out, each read as Seat N, In the room, Leaving after this hand, Away, Co-dealer or Co-dealer, not here), each with a three-dot glass button opening an `ActionSheet` with Make or Remove co-dealer where the table takes co-dealers (`TableDoor.MenuFor`; a full set answers with a toast) and Remove from table behind the existing confirm, never on the host's own row, and Waiting for players when the list is empty; the tournament card, on a free play blackjack table only; on a gil table a Gil ledger card with the trade sync switch over Taken in, Paid out and Owed (`LedgerSums` over the table ledger) that opens the ledger page, or on a chip or practice seat table a Session ledger row; and Manage table, grouped rows with gradient icon squares and a line each: Pause or Resume, Deal next round on a host-dealt table (held while paused), Co-dealers with its count (a tap scrolls to Players), Rename (an inline field and Save) and Close (red, behind a confirm whose body says anything still owed stays in My gil ledger), under the chip table note saying why Pause, Deal and co-dealers are missing, the poker note, or the Book note. A Book door has no Players section. `TableLedger` is the ledger page, titled Gil ledger on a gil table (`TableLedger.TitleFor`): the table name with refresh and Copy (plain text of the session rows and the gil entries), then on a gil table an intro line, the trade sync card and the totals card for the host, the entries as `LedgerEntryRows` (an in or out arrow, the other side's name, the kind and time, the signed amount in gold when it came in, and a RECORDED or OWED pill), and a "Missed a trade? Add it by hand" link at the bottom that opens the record form; seat tables add the session ledger table of players by buy-ins, stack and net. A tap on an entry gives the host its one action in a sheet (`CasinoGilLedger.OptionFor`): Remove on an entry the host recorded, behind a confirm sheet, Mark paid on an owed table line, and Mark unpaid on a table line the host settled; a player sees the same rows read-only under a line saying the host records them, with the night's totals (`GilLedgerTotals`, gil in and gil out over every settled entry the reader is a side of, guests included). A guest entry (`guest`, recorded for a patron without the plugin) is listed under its name with "(guest)". The record form offers the seated and registered players and, while the server lists `venue.book`, a Guest choice (the only choice at a Book, which seats nobody) with a character name field, sent through `CasinoTablesStore.RecordEntry` with `source: "manual"`; it counts at once, and each answer comes back as a toast. `GilLedgerView` (`CasinoScreen.GilLedger`, "My gil ledger") draws the same rows read-only over `GET /casino/ledger`, every entry the player is a side of from the last 30 days at open or closed tables; it opens from the Host tab, a button under the closed or removed card of a gil table or venue room, and a row in the table sheet of every gil table. The door reads itself every five seconds while drawn; when the table it shows is no longer the reader's, the table card is read again and a 404 pops every route on that table with a closed toast (the server ends a private table nobody has attached to, sat at or watched the door of for fifteen minutes).

At a host-dealt blackjack table (`BlackjackHosting.HostDeals`) the owner deals rather than plays. The server refuses them a new seat (`dealer_cannot_sit`), the empty seats on the rail stop offering Sit, and `BlackjackDeckFlow` gives them the dealer's deck instead of Sit down: Deal as the one primary once a bet is down, held disabled under "Waiting for the first bet" before that, and a short caption while the hand plays out, once it is over and while the table is paused (`BlackjackHosting.DealerDeckOf`). The row above the felt keeps Pause and Ledger and drops its own Deal for them, so Deal appears once. Co-dealers are seated players who were promoted: they keep their seat and their player deck, and Deal stays in the row above the felt for them. Deal on the door deals and opens the table, so the host lands where the hand plays out. With auto deal on the betting clock deals as at a house table, so the button on the felt, the dealer's deck and the door reads Deal now (`BlackjackHosting.DealLabel`) and deals the waiting hand early, and the dealer's deck says the table deals on the clock once a bet is down (`BlackjackHosting.TryDealerStatus`). At a gil table the owner holds the bank however it deals: every buy-in is gil traded to them and nobody can trade with themselves, so the server refuses them a new seat there too (`host_cannot_sit`). A clock-dealt gil table gives its owner the bank deck (`BlackjackHosting.BanksFromDeck`), a note that they hold the bank over the ledger button, and keeps the rail's empty seats and the ghost slots from offering Sit. A seat the owner already holds plays out.

Pause holds a practice or gil table where it stands, and the server enforces it: a blackjack table refuses bets, decisions and Deal with `table_paused`, deals nothing, runs no turn or insurance clock and auto-stands nobody until Resume, which restarts the window that was running (a whole turn for the seat on its turn). The room carries `paused` with no deadline, so every seated deck shows "The host paused the table" with only Stand (plus Rebuy for a broke seat that may rebuy, while one that cannot gets the Broke deck described with tournaments below), the state line reads Paused, the dealer's deck reads the paused caption, and the door holds Deal. A venue room fires no due timer while paused, refuses acts with `table_paused`, carries `paused` (and its current name) in its room state so the ribbon reads "The host paused the table" and the controls hold, and moves each deadline on by the pause when it resumes; a Hold'em table finishes the hand in play, deals no next one, holds its sit-out clock, and its ribbon reads the pause between hands.

The blackjack felt stacks the dealer, the state line, the seat rail and your own hands in fitted zones (`BlackjackTableLayout`) in one of two tiers, so no zone ever runs into the next. The regular tier holds only while it keeps every gap and cards at 70% or more (`RegularFitFloor`); a shorter felt, such as a tournament's or a host's, goes compact instead of shrinking the cards: the dealer puck sits left of the dealer's cards with the dealer total right of them and the shoe in the same row (the dealer's speech bubble is dropped), the rail pucks shrink and their name plates cover the lower half of the avatar with no balance title, the spots and your capsule shrink a little, and your total overlaps the foot of your cards. The compact tier then trades card size and gaps down to `CompactMinimumHeight` and, past that, `LastResortHeight`. `BlackjackTableLayout.MinimumHeight` is the regular tier's floor, every gap and card at its smallest, and everything around the felt yields to it: the hosted row keeps the bank and house rules as wrapped pills only while they fit, merges them into one scrolling line beside its buttons or on a line of its own when they do not (`HostedInfoLayout.Mode`), squeezes Pause, Deal and Ledger to share a narrow row, and a host's knock card or a refusal notice lays over the felt (`FeltTop`, `FeltBottom`) instead of squeezing it under its floor. A table seats seven (`BlackjackRules.SeatCount`), so a seated player's rail holds up to six seats and a spectator's seven: a crowded rail shrinks its spots and avatar pucks toward `SpotRadiusMin` to keep one row before it staggers alternate seats, and a staggered plate narrows (`RailPlateWidth`) so it never reaches under a neighbour's puck. Rail plates fall back to the forename when a full name does not fit and show gil in compact amounts. Every occupied rail seat shows its Perfect Pairs and 21+3 stakes as small capsules under its plate (`BlackjackTableLayout.SideStakeRect`, drawn by `DrawRailSideStakes`), gold with the net once the hand reveals a paid side bet and dimmed when it lost; your own three spots print their names inside the circles (Pairs, Bet, 21+3, `DrawSpotCaption`, fitted from `Caption1` down to `Caption2`), each name yielding to the stake or bet placed on it, and your capsule leaves room for an avatar frame (`AvatarView.Reserve`); the seat whose turn it is shows its remaining time bank as pips at the crown of its puck, inside the turn ring and clear of the stack badge under it (`BlackjackTableArt.DrawTimeBankPips` at `BlackjackTableLayout.TimeBankPips`), the same pips Hold'em draws, and blackjack spends that bank on its own when the turn clock runs out.

Knocks follow through on both sides. The host's blackjack, Hold'em and room views read the door on the same cadence and show the first waiting knock above the felt (`HostKnockBanner` over the door's own `KnockCard`). The knocker's closed card (`TableDoorCard`) runs off a `CasinoKnockStatus` in `CasinoTablesStore` (`CasinoKnocks` maps a refused attach, a knock answer and a card read to None, Waiting, Admitted, Denied or Refused): while waiting it reads the table card every three seconds, re-attaches on its own once admitted, says "Request declined" with Ask again when turned away, and titles a kicked or banned answer "Removed from this table". A waiting knock survives leaving the table and is read every fifteen seconds in the background; `CasinoKnockNotifier` tells the knocker when they are let in or turned away, and tells a host who is not on their door or table about each new knock (it polls `GET /casino/tables/{id}/knocks` every twenty seconds for the table the host owns). Joining a Knock table by code or opening a pasted invite at the door opens the table on that card.

Spectators off stops watching without a seat, never joining. An Open table with spectators off still lets anyone in while a seat is free, so the seat is picked and bought into on the felt as usual; the server shows a visitor out with `no_spectators` after two minutes without a seat and turns watchers away once the table is full. Knock and Private tables keep watching to the seated, the co-dealers and the allowlist, and venue rooms ignore the switch because everyone in them is playing.

The table's currency (`CasinoCurrencies`) decides how money moves:

- **Chips** (blackjack and Hold'em): the bank, every economy rule, standard rules only.
- **Practice** (`CasinoCurrencies.Practice`, shown to players as Free play; blackjack and Hold'em): the table never touches the bank. No sitting, rack, round, meter, XP, mission or jackpot; every seat starts at the practice stack and may rebuy when the host allows; with rebuys off, a player who stands and sits again keeps the stack they left with and is refused (`rebuy_off`) when that is below the minimum bet or the big blind. The stage shows grey chips and the practice ribbon, the cashier never opens, and hands are recorded only in the local History group. Fairness is unchanged: hands still draw from the room's seed chain and verify.
- **Gil** (blackjack and venue rooms): host-banked. The host declares a bank, a max bet and a max payout per hand, and a bet whose worst case the bank cannot cover is refused (`bank_limit`). The server never holds or converts gil; it keeps the table's gil ledger, which only the host writes. A buy-in, rebuy or payout the host records (by hand or through trade sync) settles at once, and the lines the table books itself (a cash-out, a raffle ticket or prize, a duel or dice stake) stay Owed until a trade pays them or the host marks them paid. Gil hosting runs on good faith: there is no confirmation, dispute or host reputation, and a gil table is never frozen. Venue rooms (dice table, deathroll, raffle) take buy-ins and rebuys the way a Book does, with nothing credited to a seat, so manual, guest and trade-synced buy-ins all work there and their session ledger shows entries only, no players table. Gil tables touch no coin, chip, XP, mission, club point or Hall of Fame row. Gil tables take no side bets and offer neither insurance nor surrender. Most patrons at a venue have no plugin, so the host can also book a guest: `CasinoTablesStore.RecordEntry` sends `POST /casino/tables/{id}/ledger` with a `guestName` and no counterparty, and trade sync books anyone the server cannot match the same way. A Book (`casino.book`, `CasinoWire.BookKind`) is a gil ledger with trade sync and no game, for venues that run games the app does not have: a hosted table with a bank, an optional max payout, a listing and a location, no seats and no room socket, exempt from the empty-table sweep and ended by the server eight idle hours after its last door read or entry (`CasinoHostingRules.BookIdleHours`). Both ride on the `venue.book` feature flag (`CasinoFeatures.VenueBook`); an older server shows no guest option, no Book poster and no Book in the host sheet.

`CasinoCurrencies.SeatBanked(currency)` is true for practice and gil: the stage balance is the seat's own stack, not the bankroll.

## Venue layer

Hosted venue rooms (`casino.dice-table`, `casino.deathroll`, `casino.raffle`) run on practice chips or gil, open from Live, the Host tab, the door or a deep link into `CasinoScreen.VenueRoom`, and are drawn by `Venue/VenueCabinet.cs` on the Strip with a room ribbon. Each room has a Dalamud-free playback (`DiceTablePlayback`, `DeathrollPlayback`, `RafflePlayback`) that takes the room's GameState and choreographs only what the server returned; a mid-event join snaps to the current state. `CasinoVenueStore` parses the GameState off the draw path (`VenueRoomView`), sends `POST /casino/venue/{roomId}/act` with an idempotent `clientActionId`, verifies the last roll or draw through `GET /casino/rooms/{roomId}/verify/{seq}` (`VenueVerifier` over `VenueDraws`, pinned by `venue.json`), polls `GET /casino/tables/nearby` from the housing position (`IHousingPositionSource`), and when a nearby poll returns a listed table the directory (`tables.Listed`, refreshed on the 60 s foreground and 120 s background cadence) does not hold yet, asks `CasinoTablesStore.RefreshNow` once per new table id so the table's card is known within the nearby cadence of 15 to 30 s. The Venues app carries no Gamba pill: Live already lists every table at the plot first, and the venue directories (FFXIVVenues, Partake) list no Japanese worlds, so a pill keyed by venue address could never show there. A dice table running highest-roll rounds takes one roll per player per open round: the server refuses a repeat with `already_rolled`, the roll button holds and reads You rolled this round once your id is in the round's rollers, and the Recent rolls log scrolls in its own child through the 50 rolls the room keeps. A round closes as soon as everyone attached to the room has rolled (`DiceTableHandler.EveryoneRolled` over the members the clock hands the step), so two players never wait out a long clock; a member who sits a round out leaves it to its clock. Free rolling and rolls between rounds stay unlimited. A dice table hosted for gil can carry a stake per round (`CasinoDiceTableOptionsDto.Stake`: the `##hostStake` ladder under Die sides in the host sheet, with a No stake rung, offered only with highest-roll rounds, and `VenueRules.CheckDice` mirrors the server's check): when a staked round's clock runs out the server writes a `round.close` line and holds the stakes the way a deathroll does: every roller below the winner owes the host the stake and the host owes the winner the stake times the number of losers, each an owed `stake` line (a host who rolled is left out of their own lines), and the room reads "Stake {0} gil" under its round line (`DiceTableRoom.DrawStakeLine`). The Host again card names the stake like deathroll's. A raffle's buy row takes a ticket count (`RaffleTicketPicker`: a free-input field with fewer, more and Max, default 1, clamped to the tickets left to that person) and shows the total before buying; one act buys the lot (`CasinoVenueActRequest.Count`, which an old client leaves at 0 for one ticket), the draw still gives each ticket its own chance, and a gil raffle writes one `Ticket` ledger entry of count times price that a single trade settles; a further purchase by the same buyer grows that entry while it is still owed, so Buy 1 tapped five times is still one line. The room's info line and the host sheet's raffle step show the per-person, winner and entrant caps (`VenueRules`), and the server answers `ticket_count` for a count past what is left, keeping `ticket_limit` for a buyer already at the maximum or a full raffle. The host picks the per-person limit when opening a raffle, up to 1,000 (`VenueRules.MaxTicketsPerPerson`, four digits in the composer and the buy row). Before the draw the host or a co-dealer opens `RaffleEntrantsSheet` from the deck (Entrants above Draw now at a gil raffle, where the host has no buy row, and a secondary pill beside Draw now otherwise) and removes an entrant with `raffle.remove` (`CasinoVenueActRequest.TargetUserId`) after a confirm: their name and tickets leave the raffle, and at a gil raffle their unpaid ticket line is struck off and a paid one becomes a refund the host owes, the way closing the room voids tickets, counting only tickets bought since their last removal. Removing someone from the room (`/kick`) writes the same removal, so a kicked patron never stays in the draw, and a removed entrant may take tickets again.

The Host tab's poster grid follows `VenuePosters`: Blackjack, Hold'em, Dice table, Deathroll, Raffle, and a sixth, Book, while `venue.book` is open (`VenuePosters.Count`). Each poster carries a plays-for line from `VenuePosters.MetaOf` over `HostGames.Accepts` (gil, free play or chips; chips or free play; gil or free tickets for the Raffle; "Gil, any game" for the Book; or the lines without gil when gil tables are closed) and a Gil badge while `gil.tables` is open; a tap opens the host sheet on that game with gil preselected when it takes gil. The Your table card appears when the account owns an open hosted table, venue room or Book (`CasinoTablesStore.HostedTableId`), and Host again when a draft is remembered for the character and the server still lists its game (`HostDraft.Reopens`).

The stage Extra button on blackjack tables and venue rooms opens `VenueTableSheet`: verify, the trade sync switch (for the host of a gil table only), the broadcast view for spectators and the report action (`Plugin.Report`, target `casino_table`); its room code card is sized by `ScrollLayout.NativeScrollContentWidth`, like every other native-scroll casino sheet, so the scrollbar never resizes it. While the owner views their own table (blackjack, Hold'em or a venue room), the stage top bar carries a Host pill (`CasinoStageSpec.Host`: rose with a sliders icon and a gold badge counting waiting knocks, `HostKnocks`) that pops back to the door when the door is below and opens it otherwise (`OpenHostControls`); when the label would squeeze the chips capsule under `CasinoStageLayout.CapsuleMinimum` it collapses to a glass circle (`CasinoStageLayout.PlaceHost`). Backing out of a table onto its own door keeps the seat and the room (`CasinoRoute.KeepsSeat`), the door's Go to table goes back to it (`CasinoRoute.ReturnsToTable`), and the seat is only left when the door itself is closed. A practice deathroll shows your stack above its deck, and the newest finished duel keeps its rolls so the losing 1 lands on screen and verifies. Live lists a venue room by everyone in it, since it has no seats.

An admitted patron opening a live Book ledger first joins through `POST /casino/tables/{id}/ledger/join`, a Book-only admission check that records their membership without creating a seat or a gil entry. `BookLedgerAdmission` keeps one request per visit and discards answers after a table or account switch. The client holds the ledger behind loading, retry or the existing door state until the join succeeds; hosts and historical ledger reads keep their existing access. This gives the server a table-scoped roster for the first trade even though a Book has no room socket. The backend must support this endpoint; an unavailable endpoint remains retryable rather than silently treating registration as successful.

Trade sync is opt-in (`Configuration.CasinoHostTradeSync`, a new name so a player who turned the old player-side switch on starts with it off) and host-only in effect: the switch shows only to the host of a gil table, and the server records a trade only for an account with an open gil table. `TradeWindowReader` samples arrays only, every frame while the switch is on: whether the `Trade` addon is visible, the partner from `TradeStringArray.TradePartnerName` (the text payloads before the first non-text payload are the name, the text after it is the home world), both offers from `TradeNumberArray` (`SendGilCount` mine, `ReceiveGilCount` theirs) and the wallet. `TradeSyncTracker` (Dalamud-free, pinned by `TradeSyncTests`) keeps one snapshot per trade with the latest partner and offers. Closing the window arms it for 30 seconds (four at most, the oldest drops), and a window that opens again with the same partner while that snapshot is armed continues it instead of starting a second trade. Each frame's wallet is compared with the last frame's: a change completes the oldest armed or open snapshot whose offers net to exactly it. A trade with no gil offered never completes, and any other change (a vendor, the market board, a teleport fee) and any frame whose wallet could not be read completes nothing.

`CasinoTradeSync` sends each completion to `POST /casino/ledger/trade` (`CasinoLedgerTradeRequest`: a fresh `clientTradeId`, the partner's name and world, the gil received and sent, and as a hint the host's own gil table on screen, else empty), one request at a time in order, and retries a missing answer with the same id after 2, 5 and 10 seconds before posting a Trade not recorded notification. The server picks the host's open gil table, matches the name against the people at it (the world only breaks a tie; gil the host sends also reaches a gil table they closed in the last 12 hours, so cash-outs paid after closing land), and settles an owed line of exactly that amount, splits the oldest larger one, or writes a settled buy-in, rebuy or payout, a guest entry when nobody matches; at gil blackjack a buy-in credits the seat or waits for the next sit, and a payout comes off the stack. A granted answer is patched straight into that table's cached ledger and raises a notification and `TradeRecordedCard` ("{kind} recorded", the amount and partner, Undo and OK, gone after eight seconds). Undo and an entry's Remove call `POST /casino/ledger/{entryId}/remove`, and Mark paid calls `/settle`. `no_gil_table` and `config_invalid` are only logged. Every decision is logged under `[TradeSync]` with amounts and outcomes, never names, and nothing in the game is automated.

`CasinoTablesStore` caches one ledger per table id, each with its own loaded and attempted ticks and in-flight flag: a ledger page re-reads every five seconds while drawn, a failed read retries after three, a refresh asked for while one is in flight runs again when it lands, `UpsertLedgerEntry` and `RemoveLedgerEntry` patch the cache and the personal ledger at once, and an account switch clears it all. A table card asked for while another card loads is fetched when that one lands, so a new table's door shows its ledger right away.

`BroadcastView` (`CasinoScreen.Broadcast`, landscape) projects the blackjack or Hold'em snapshot into `BroadcastTable` with large seats, stacks and cards and no controls. When the table ends the broadcast clears and says why instead of holding its last frame. `TournamentOverlay` draws the practice blackjack tournament strip, leaderboard, eliminations and winner over the table; the standings open once when the winner is called and close on a tap on the strip or the board, and both claim their area so the felt underneath never takes the tap. Hosts start and stop it from `TournamentDoorCard` on the door, which holds Start until two players are seated, says when the host still has to sit or deals the table and so cannot enter, and checks the starting stack and the hand count before sending: Hands is parsed as typed (blank means 20), and a count outside 10 to 50 disables Start under a line naming the band, which the placeholder names too; the server names those refusals `tournament_players`, `tournament_stack` and `tournament_hands`, and coin and gil doors leave the tournament card out, since only practice tables run tournaments. Start pressed while a hand is in play is not refused: during the results intermission the hand has already settled, so the tournament opens at once for the next hand; otherwise the server queues it and answers `tournament_pending` as granted, the door reads "Starts after this hand" (`CasinoTableDoorDto.TournamentPending`) with Stop taking the pending start back, and the tournament opens inside that hand's settlement with every seat reset to the starting stack, or is dropped when fewer than two seats remain. A finished tournament keeps its strip and standings until the next hand settles or the host presses Stop, which clears a finished record instead of refusing it, and a player dismisses the finished strip by closing the standings board (`TournamentOverlay.Showing`, an instance the table owns). The winner banner wraps (`GameBanner.DrawWrapped`) and no elimination notice is drawn over it. A seat below the minimum bet with nothing bet gets the Broke deck: Stand, the ledger on a gil table, and a line saying it is out of the tournament, that rebuys pause during a tournament, or that the host turned rebuys off. With rebuys off, a practice seat that stands and sits again keeps the stack it left with, and one that left below the minimum bet is refused with `rebuy_off`. The server takes an entrant who leaves a live tournament out of it and refuses them a new seat (`tournament_live`), so while you are still in one (`BlackjackHosting.StillInTournament`) the deck's leave button reads Resign and asks through `BlackjackTable.ConfirmResign`. Every other held seat asks "Leave the table?" first (`TableLeaveConfirm`): blackjack's Leave pill, which sits where Hit appears when a turn starts, Hold'em's Leave icon, the stage back button and the door's back button (when the door is all that holds the seat) all go through `BlackjackTable.ConfirmLeave` or `HoldemTable.ConfirmLeave`, with Keep playing as the way out. Going back from the table to its own door keeps the seat and asks nothing.

## Adding a game

The server ships the game first: its kind, routes, feature flag, and a vector file when the game is seeded. Then, on the client:

1. **Ids.** Add the game id to `Apps/Casino/CasinoGames.cs` and the wire kind to `Core/Casino/CasinoWire.cs`. Add the flag to `CasinoFeatures`, `CasinoFeatureSet.Known` and `CasinoGameGate.FlagFor`.
2. **Wire.** Add the DTOs to a `Casino*Dtos.cs` file (every field defaulted) and register them in `AethernetJsonContext`; add the routes to `CasinoClient` (a new partial file for a new area); pin route and JSON shape in a `*WireContractTests` class. New refusal reasons go into `CasinoReasons` with a string and a `CasinoReasonCoverageTests` entry.
3. **Rules and vectors.** Mirror the pay tables and draws in `Core/Casino/<Game>Rules.cs` with tests against the backend constants. Copy the backend vector file verbatim into `src/Aetherphone.Tests/Vectors/`, pin its SHA-256 and replay it. Teach `CasinoVerifier` the game's draw purposes.
4. **Store.** Extend `CasinoPlayStore` for a solo game or `CasinoRoomsStore` for a room; results come back through `Take*`, never through a callback into Draw.
5. **Cabinet.** A class under its own `Apps/Casino/<Game>/` folder with `Draw(stage, frame, ui)`, a `CasinoStageSpec`, a playback class, `BetComposer` for every stake, `stage.Celebration` for every win, `stage.Settle` for every settled round, and `ICabinetIdle`. Follow the full screen and legibility rules above.
6. **Host wiring.** In `CasinoApp`: the cabinet field, its case in `DrawStage`, its spec in `StageSpecFor`, its branch in `ResetCabinetOf` (check after merges that no branch ends in a doubled `return;`), and `StageBalance` if it shows a stack other than the bankroll.
7. **Lobby.** A `StripCatalog` entry on the right shelf (Cards, Machines or Quick bets; a clocked room goes on `CasinoApp.LiveRooms` and the live rail instead of a shelf), a `CasinoSign` word, the name in `CasinoGameNames`, the rules sheet steps and pitch in `CasinoRules`, a glyph in `CasinoGlyphs`, an accent in `CasinoArt`, the minimum bet and printed return in `CasinoApp.Floor`, and the mission sentence in `MissionText`. A shelf game joins the Jump back in rail by itself once it has been played; `CasinoFloorLayoutTests` pins that every playable id is reached exactly once.
8. **Copy.** Every string as a `LocString` in its own nested class in `L.cs` plus all nine JSONs. Game names never take an "Aether" prefix.
