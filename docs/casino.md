# Gamba

Gamba is the casino app (app id `casino`, route prefix `/casino`, key prefix `casino.`). Coins become chips at the cashier, and those chips play every house game on one floor-wide bankroll. Hosted tables can also run on practice chips or, for venues, on gil banked by the host. This page describes the client as it ships: the economy it mirrors, the stores, the stage kit every play screen runs on, the cabinets and their playback, hosting and the venue layer, the lobby, and how to add a game. The HTTP routes and realtime room kinds are listed in [Networking](networking.md#casino-routes).

The server decides and the client performs. Every outcome is drawn by the server from a committed seed; the client only choreographs a result it has already been sent (reels decelerate onto it, the ball bounces along its path, the race script ends in its order) and never fabricates a near miss.

## Economy on the client

The client mirrors the economy for display only; every amount that moves money is checked by the server again.

- **Rate.** `CasinoChipLots.ChipPerCoin` is 1,000 chips per coin, both ways. Chips stay a currency of their own; every chip balance also prints its coin value (`ChipValue.Coins`, cached by `ChipValueText`).
- **Buying chips.** `POST /casino/chips/buy {coins, clientActionId}` opens a bankroll or tops up the live one in one idempotent call (`CasinoStore.BuyChips`), from 1 coin up to the whole wallet; a short wallet answers `insufficient`. The answer carries the new stack, the wallet balance and the ceiling, which the store absorbs before the next frame.
- **Cashing out.** `POST /casino/sittings/close` converts the whole bankroll in one call, whole coins, with the sub-coin residue kept by the house. There is no daily cash-out limit and no buy-in limit; a blackjack rack keeps its own table band (`CasinoHostingRules.ChipMinBuyIn` to `ChipMaxBuyIn`, or the table's `minBuyIn` and `maxBuyIn`).
- **Max win per bet.** `GET /casino` sends `maxWinPerBet` (chips, policy data, 50,000 coins by default). The server clamps the net win of every house-banked round to it and marks the round `capped`; Hold'em pots and the floor jackpot never meet it. The info sheet and the pay tables print it through `MaxWinText`, and a capped result passes `Capped` in its `CasinoBetRecord`, which shows "Max win per bet reached" on the stage.
- **Ladder and ceiling.** `CasinoLadder` mirrors the global ladder (27 rungs from 100 to 50B), `LevelCap(level)` (geometric between the anchors, level 1 at 10K up to level 100 at 50B) and `MaxBet(level, balance, anchors, maxWin) = min(max(LevelCap, balance / 20), maxWin / 10)` floored to a rung (`MaxWinBetMultiple`), so every win up to 10x at the ceiling pays in full and the max win clamp only meets the long tail. The ceiling's `reason` is `level`, `balance` or `max_win`. `CasinoStore.Ceiling` returns the server's `ceiling` block when present and falls back to the local formula against an older floor. Every player-chosen stake snaps to the ladder; the server refuses the rest with `ladder` or `ceiling`.
- **Bonuses.** `CasinoBonuses` claim `welcome`, `timed`, `reload`, `streak`, `levelup`, `broke` and `rebate` through `POST /casino/bonus/{kind}/claim`, from the Floor hero and the missions sheet. Grants land as chips in the bankroll (opening one when none is live).
- **Levels, club, titles.** `GET /casino` carries `progress` (level, XP bounds, lifetime figures, balance title) and `club` (tier, points, multiplier, rebate rate). `LevelCapsule`, `StatusTitle` (Shark 1M, High Roller 10M, VIP 100M, Whale 1B, Legend 1T) and the club capsule and sheet render them; the client computes none of it.
- **Feature flags.** `GET /casino` sends `features[]`, parsed once into a `CasinoFeatureSet` (`CasinoFeatures` holds the names: `economy.v3`, `bonus`, `levels`, `club`, `hosting.v2`, `venue`, `gil.tables`, `roomcodes`, `machines`, `missions`, `challenges`, `fame`, `feed`, `rain`, `holdem`, `race`, `plinko`, `originals`, `venue.book`). `CasinoGameGate.FlagFor(gameId)` maps a game to its flag, and a game whose flag is missing shows a "Not open yet" tile instead of opening, so a new client degrades cleanly against an older server.
- **Limits.** The self-set daily loss limit stays opt-in (0 on the wire means no limit); see the gotcha in [Networking](networking.md#gotchas).

## Stores

Every store follows the house pattern: requests run off the draw thread through `StoreWork`, results are parked in a field and the draw path takes them once with a `Take*` method (`TakeSpinResult`, `TakeSeatOutcome`, `TakeFailure`), so Draw never awaits and never allocates for a result. Built in `PhoneServices`.

| Store | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| `CasinoStore` | `GET /casino` (state, sittings, features, ceiling, max win, bonuses, club, progress), one-call chip buys, cash-out, limits, bonus claims, the per-character Auto top-up switch |
| `CasinoPlayStore` | Solo machine routes: slot spins, the Golden Bird gamble, Moogle Money meters, scratch, Barkeep, and the pending-round recovery (`PendingCasinoRound`, per character); owns `Originals` (`CasinoOriginalsStore`) and `Plinko` (`CasinoPlinkoStore`) |
| `CasinoRoomsStore` | The room list, the one live `CasinoRoomSession` (attach, snapshot, events with seq and epoch, private lanes, clock skew), wheel and race bets and bingo cards |
| `CasinoTablesStore` | The table browser, quick seat, blackjack seats, hosting (create, door, invites, kick, rename, co-dealers, pause, deal, tournament, close), the session ledger, nearby tables |
| `HoldemStore` | Hold'em seats and actions, time bank, sit out, top-up, hand history, the rejoin penalty |
| `CasinoVenueStore` | Venue room GameState (`VenueRoomView`), venue acts, roll and raffle verification, tournaments, trade-sync proposals |
| `CasinoFloorStore` | Missions and claims, challenges, Hall of Fame boards, the bets feed, the `casino-floor` watch with its ticker and rain notes |
| `CasinoHistoryStore` | History pages, round verification, and hosted table hands recorded locally (practice and gil hands never reach the server's history) |
| `CasinoSpinStore` | The free daily spin, which pays wallet coins rather than chips |

`CasinoRoomSession` parses the snapshot's GameState into one typed slot per kind (`Wheel`, `Bingo`, `Blackjack`, `Race`, `Holdem`); venue GameState is parsed by `CasinoVenueStore`. A sequence gap or an epoch change triggers a resync. A `restarting` or `draining` answer (a deploy) or an attach `cooldown` keeps the room and its last state instead of closing it: the session shows the reconnect veil, attaches again on the next connection and retries on its own every four seconds while the socket stays up. A room read that answers not found asks the table card why before it closes the room, so a player outside the door sees Ask to join (or the waiting, denied or removed card) rather than an ended table. `CasinoSeatMachine` and `CasinoJoinGate` hold the seat states and the "joins next hand" rules shared by the tables, and `CasinoTurnNotifier` posts a notification when your turn comes while the table is not in view.

## Stage frame

Every play screen draws inside one host frame, `CasinoStage`. The lobby, cashier, history, fairness and limits pages keep the normal app chrome; cabinets, tables and rooms go full bleed with no `AppHeader`.

The host (`CasinoApp.DrawStage`) opens `AppSurface.BeginEdgeToEdge(content, true)` and then, per frame:

```csharp
var frame = stage.Begin(area, spec, balance, casino.Ceiling);
cabinet.Draw(stage, frame, ui);
var action = stage.End(ui);
```

`Begin` draws the backdrop and the frosted deck, advances effects, and returns a `CasinoStageFrame`: `Layout`, `Safe`, `Deck`, `Body`, `Full`, `DeltaSeconds`, `Phase` (the lights clock), `Instant`, `Focused`, `Blocked` (a reality check is showing) and `SnapToTruth`. `End` draws particles, the celebration, screen effects, the practice ribbon, the reality-check card, the bets handle and the chrome, and returns `CasinoStageAction.Back` or `Cashier` for the host to act on. Overlays draw at window level through `stage.Gate()` and `stage.DrawOverlays(screen, ui)`; `TakeInfoRequest()` returns `Rules`, `Extra` or `Fairness`, and `TakeRoundRequest()` a round id tapped in the bets rail.

`CasinoStageSpec(GameId, Title, Preset, Room, DeckHeight, Practice, BetsRail, InstantAvailable, ReturnTenths, Extra, Warmth, LampPool)` declares the screen: `Room` adds the 40 unit phase ribbon, `DeckHeight` reserves the bet deck (0 for layouts that draw their own controls), `Practice` swaps the capsule to grey Practice chips and adds the practice ribbon, `ReturnTenths` prints the return in the info sheet, `Extra` names a second info button (a pay table, odds sheet or the table sheet), `History` names a recent hands button (blackjack), `Warmth` tints the Strip toward amber, `LampPool` lights the felt. `CasinoApp.StageSpecFor(route)` picks the spec per screen.

Geometry, in design units times `UiScale.Current`: chrome band 52; back chip and info chip 36 glass circles centred 28 in from either side at y 26; the chips capsule centred on the band; ribbon 40 and practice ribbon 22 under the band; deck at the bottom across the full screen width (`BetComposer.DeckHeightFor(knob, fixedAmount)` returns exactly its rows); `Safe` sits between them, inset 12. Worlds fit inside `Safe`; lights and backdrops use `Full`. `CasinoStageLayout.ChromeContains(point)` tells a cabinet whether a press landed on the chrome.

The band holds the back chip, the chips capsule and the info chip and nothing else. The capsule is fitted in `Begin` (`ChromeCapsule.Fit`, exposed as `stage.Capsule`): it drops the full coin value, then the compact one, before it would outgrow the room between the two chips. Room status (timers, crowd, history strips) goes under the band, in the ribbon a spec reserves with `Room: true` (`Layout.Ribbon`). In landscape (`Layout.Landscape`) the band is the back chip alone. Short notices such as "Max win per bet reached" sit just above the deck through `Layout.Notice(width, height, scale)`, never over a room's status line.

Snap to truth: when the stage was not drawn for more than 2 seconds, or the phone regains focus after more than 2 seconds away, `frame.SnapToTruth` is true for one frame, the balance and the celebration snap to their final state, and cabinets finish their own animations.

`stage.RepeatPressed()` reports Space while the stage has focus, no overlay is open and no text field is active, claiming only the Space key through `GameInput`.

## Full screen and legibility

Binding for every cabinet, table and room:

1. The game is the screen. The world (felt, track, reels, board, wheel) fills the stage edge to edge under the glass chrome. No boxed play area, no inset panel, no `GameScene.Arena`. Felt tables are the backdrop itself, with seats sitting on it.
2. Night, never bright. Backdrops stay at or under 0.22 luminance; no bright saturated full-screen fills. Text sits on a dark backdrop or on glass.
3. Readable text. Body copy at least `Subheadline`, status lines at least `Footnote` in strong ink (`StageText.Strong`), amounts at least `Title3`, the primary state line ("Place your bets", "Your turn") at least `Title2` with a soft shadow. Muted ink only on glass or dark felt and only for secondary labels. Contrast at least 4.5:1 against the brightest point behind the text.
4. One obvious next action: one full-width primary in the bet deck, secondary actions as smaller pills beside it. Empty seats are large Sit spots, not small "Open" circles.
5. Use the space: worlds grow to fill Safe, nothing smaller than a 44 unit touch target.
6. Clean entry: one tap from the Floor into any game, Back returns to where you came from, rules one tap away on the info chip.
7. Every idle state has motion and every state change has feedback.

### Stage text

`StageText` is the one way to put words on the world. Each helper resolves the requested style up to its role minimum (`StageText.Minimum(role)`, never shrinking below it) and either fits or marquees against its container:

- `State(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id)`: Title2 or larger in strong ink with a soft shadow; overflow moves onto a glass capsule and marquees. `StateLine(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, ink)` is the id-free form that ellipsizes.
- `Status(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id, overWorld)`: Footnote or larger in strong ink, on a dark glass capsule when it sits over the world. `Status(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, scale)` and `Plate(..., ink, style, scale)` are the id-free capsule forms.
- `Amount(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id)`: Title3 or larger in gold.
- `Label(drawList, center, text, maxWidth, id, muted)`: Footnote or larger; a muted label always sits on the capsule.
- `FitScale(text, maxWidth, style, role)`: the shrink-to-fit scale clamped at the role minimum.

`StageText.CapsuleFill` is dark enough that muted ink reads at 4.5:1 even over the brightest felt; `StageContrast.Ratio` and `Over` are the WCAG math the tests use.

### Felt tables, seats and the action row

`FeltTable.DrawCloth(drawList, full, rail, practice, scale)` paints the night cloth over any rect (a grey cloth for practice tables) for scenes that are not on a Felt stage, such as idle previews and Hold'em's own felt. A `FeltTable` instance draws the table layer over a Felt stage with `Draw(drawList, full, table, new FeltTableOptions(seats, print, cloth, rail, circles, practice), scale)`: a gold insurance arc, betting circles and an optional printed line such as "BLACKJACK PAYS 3 TO 2" set along an arc in low-contrast gold, and returns `FeltTableGeometry` (dealer anchor at the top centre, the seat arc, `Seat(index)`, `BettingCircle(index)`), all scaled to the table rect with seats of at least 28 units radius.

`SeatSpot.DrawEmpty(drawList, center, radius, label, accent, invite, scale)` is the empty seat: a dark disc of at least 56 units across with a plus glyph and a "Sit" label, pulsing a glow while `invite` is set (you are not seated), returning true on a tap.

`DeckActions.Row(deck, scale)` is the bottom row of the bet deck, 56 units tall and full width; every other deck row is 44 (`DeckActions.SecondaryHeight`) with 8 between rows and 12 of side padding. `stage.SecondaryAction(label, enabled, ink)` lays out up to two pills from the left and `stage.PrimaryAction(label, enabled, ink)` takes the rest, the whole row when there are none. Pills are as wide as their measured label plus 14 a side (`DeckActions.PillWidth`, 44 minimum) and are never cut: the primary gives way from 140 down to an 88 floor first. A row with more pills than it can hold goes on one pannable rail through `DeckActions.Rail(chipRail, row, ui, labels, active)`; `DeckActions.DrawPill` and `DrawIconPill` draw single content-sized pills. `DeckActions.Above` places a status line over the row, and `DeckActions.Slice` splits it for two equal choices such as Insure and No insurance.

## Lights, signs and colours

`CasinoLights` is static and allocation free: `BulbChase(drawList, rect, radius, scale, phase, spacing, colorA, colorB, lit)` (6 unit bulbs on a 14 unit pitch, every third dark, chasing at 6 bulbs a second), `NeonTube(drawList, path, color, width, glow)`, `Spotlight(drawList, origin, direction, length, spread, color, alpha)`, `Bokeh(drawList, rect, phase, density, scale)`, `CoinShower(scale)` and `CoinShowerEmitter(scale, rate)` (gold discs, gravity 180 units, a pulse size curve), `Sparkle`, `Shard`, `Ring` particle specs, and `LightSweep(backdrop, strength)`. `CasinoSigns.Draw(drawList, CasinoSign, center, height, color, lit)` strokes a sign in neon (`HeightToFit` sizes it to a box); a new game adds its word to the `CasinoSign` enum and the word table. `CasinoColors` holds `Money`, `MoneyHighlight`, `LightA` (rose), `LightB` (cyan), the felt pair, the warm inks, `Practice` and `Loss`. `AppPalettes.Gamba` is the app palette; the casino accent is `AccentRing.Rose`.

## Win celebrations

`stage.Celebration.Celebrate(stake, payout, origin, instant, jackpot)` picks a tier from the net win over the stake (`WinLadder.TierFor`): Win above 0, Nice from 3x, Big from 10x, Mega from 25x, Epic from 50x, Legendary from 100x or any jackpot. A payout at or below the stake returns `WinTier.None` and does nothing: no light, no particles, no sound, the paying symbols simply light. Each tier carries its sparkles or confetti, coin shower seconds, light sweep, banner, bulb chase, full-screen card, sound and count-up seconds (`WinLadder.Spec`). Big and up flash, punch, vignette or slow the game. A tap on Safe skips after half a second; Instant mode shows the final amount with a 0.3 second pop. `Blocking` is true while an Epic or Legendary card covers the stage. `WinCelebration` is the only celebration path in the app.

`CasinoSfx.Play(sound)`, `Pitched(sound, step)` and `Win(spec)` play only while the stage is drawn and focused, and WinBig, WinEpic and Fanfare share one 10 second slot (a throttled big win falls back to WinSmall). The casino cues (`UiSound.ReelTick` through `LevelUp`) are synthesized by tools/sound-generator (`python generate-sounds.py --casino`).

## Bet composer and the ceiling

`BetComposer.Draw(stage, ui, frame.Deck, model, delta)` returns `Confirm`, `StartAuto`, `StopAuto` or `None`. `BetComposerModel(MinimumBet, MaximumBet, Stack, Action, Enabled, AutoAvailable, FixedAmount, Knob, Repeat, Busy, Cost, Rack)`: `MaximumBet` is the game's own cap clamped to `casino.Ceiling.MaxBet`; `Cost` is what the action takes from the stack when that differs from the amount (an ante, a bonus buy, a Dealer Hold'em deal); `Rack` marks a table rack, the one stack the composer clamps to; `Action` is a one-argument template ("Buy {0}", "Bet {0}") that receives the compact amount; `Knob` reserves `composer.KnobRect` (a 44 unit row) for the game's own control (risk, rows, mines, target, runner, spot, card count); `FixedAmount` hides the amount row. The amount row (`DeckAmountRow`) is the field plus the quick chips "½", "2×" and the localized Max, each sized to its label; the field keeps an 88 unit floor and "½" then "2×" step aside on rows too narrow for all three. The field taps into free input and commits on Enter or blur; every value snaps to the ladder through `CasinoLadder.Clamp`, `Half`, `Double` and `Top`. With `AutoAvailable` the action row starts with a content-sized Auto pill (repeat icon and label, folding to the icon on a narrow row) that opens the auto settings sheet (`composer.Gate()` and `composer.DrawOverlay(screen, ui, bonusAvailable)`); the primary is always the manual action. The sheet's own "Start auto" button starts the run on the next frame (`AutoBetPlan.RouteStart` starts a covered run, tops the stack up when auto top-up can, or opens Get chips) and returns `StartAuto`; while it runs the primary reads "Stop (12 left)" and returns `StopAuto`. `composer.AutoArmed` stays true from opening the sheet until the next manual bet (Mines plans its auto picks then). `composer.Auto` is the `AutoBetPlan`: the cabinet starts the next round with `Auto.Next` while `Auto.Running`, and reports each settled round with `Auto.Settle(stake, payout, bonus, min, max, stack)`, which applies on-win and on-loss adjustments and stops on count, profit, loss, bonus or chips. The info sheet prints the ceiling, its reason and the max win per bet.

**Get chips.** A bankroll bet is not clamped to the stack: when its cost is above the stack (or there is no bankroll), the primary action becomes Get chips through `ChipsDesk.Primary`, which opens `GetChipsSheet` over the stage. The sheet shows the wallet, the chips with their coin value, three amounts sized to the bet (`ChipsAmounts.ForNeed`: enough for 10, 50 and 100 bets, or 1, 2 and 5 buy-ins at a table, rounded up to whole coins and clamped to the wallet), a custom amount and the Auto top-up switch (`Configuration.CasinoAutoTopUp`, per character, off by default). With Auto top-up on, `AutoTopUpRule` buys enough for ten bets when the wallet covers the bet, and the composer places the bet once the chips land; auto play asks `composer.Covers(stage, need, stack)` before each round. A refused buy rests auto top-up for 30 seconds. Every deck that is not a composer (the race deck, the blackjack and Hold'em sit decks) calls `ChipsDesk.DrawGetChips` for the same action, so the cashier is never the only way in. Every buy toasts "Bought {chips} chips for {coins} coins", or notes it in the cashier when that is open.

Gil is not on the ladder, so gil blackjack tables bet through `ClassicBetComposer`: the same amount row (a free gil field with ½, 2× and Max) over a 56 unit primary, bounded by the host's limits and the seat's stack. The race deck (`RaceDeck` over `RaceDeckLayout`) shares the amount row under its bet kind switch, labels its button "Bet 100" and shows the pick ("Saffron Dash · 5.62x", from `RaceDeck.Caption`) or the prompt as a fitted caption just above it. Hold'em raises use `HoldemRaiseComposer` (Min, half pot, three quarters, Pot and All in as 44 unit chips on a pannable rail, a slider and a one big blind stepper, the button label always the exact amount).

Every settled chip round goes through `stage.Settle(new CasinoBetRecord(game, stake, payout, roundId, settledAtUnixMs, capped))`, which feeds the bets rail's My bets tab and the reality check (`RealityCheck`: a card every 100 rounds or 30 minutes of play showing rounds, minutes and session net, with Keep playing or Take a break). The bets rail's All bets and High rollers tabs read `GET /casino/feed`.

## Playback

A playback class turns a server answer into choreography and is the only thing a cabinet animates from. The shape is the same everywhere: `Begin` or `Update` takes the DTO (or the room state plus the server clock), `Advance` moves time, the cabinet drains cues with `TryTake*` or `Take*` (a tick, a landing, a reveal, a settle) to fire sounds, particles and the celebration, and `Snap` or a snap flag jumps to the final state for a mid-event join, a reconnect or `frame.SnapToTruth`. Playbacks hold no ImGui state, so the tests drive them directly (`*PlaybackTests`). Client-side flourishes that need randomness (a ball's wobble, a patron's walk, commentary lines) draw from `GameRandom` seeded from the round, so the same round replays identically.

| Playback | Replays |
| --- | --- |
| `MachineRoundPlayback`, `MachineRollup` | A spin's `steps[]` beat by beat (spin, tumble, expand, hold, respin, collect, meter), then the rollup at half the bet a second up to 20x |
| `PlinkoFlight` | Each ball along its server `path`, a scripted bounce per row, up to ten balls in flight |
| `DiceRollPlayback`, `LimboClimbPlayback`, `KenoDrawPlayback` | The instant originals: the sliding roll marker, the climbing multiplier, drawn tiles in sequence (Mines and Hi-Lo keep their open round in `MinesBoard` and `HiLoChain`) |
| `ScratchCardPlayback` | The foil reveal of a bought ticket |
| `WheelRoundPlayback`, `WheelChoreography`, `WheelPointer` | The wheel landing on the server's segment |
| `BingoRoundPlayback` | Called balls, flights, daubs and stage wins (a burst of balls after a join jumps straight to the board) |
| `RaceRoundPlayback` | The race from `RaceScript`, rebuilt from the order and seed revealed at Locked |
| `BlackjackDealPlayback`, `BlackjackDealChoreography` | Card flights, the hole card reveal and per-seat settlement |
| `HoldemPlayback` | Hole cards, board streets, reveals at showdown and chips sweeping to winners |
| `DailySpinPlayback` | The sprung pointer of the free spin |
| `DiceTablePlayback`, `DeathrollPlayback`, `RafflePlayback`, `TournamentPlayback` | Venue rolls, duels, the raffle draw and the blackjack tournament strip |

## Rules mirrors, vectors and fairness

Core/Casino holds one Dalamud-free mirror per game (`BlackjackRules`, `BlackjackSideBets`, `HoldemRules`, `HoldemHands`, `RaceRules`, `RaceScript`, `PlinkoRules`, `OriginalsRules`, `SlotsRules`, `SlotsMachines`, `GoldenBirdRules`, `CrystalCascadeRules`, `MoogleMoneyRules`, `ScratchRules`, `WheelRules`, `BingoRules`, `BarkeepRules`, `DailySpinRules`, `VenueRules`, `CasinoHostingRules`, `CasinoFloorRules`, `CasinoLadder`). They exist to print pay tables, returns, bands and odds, and to replay draws for verification; they never decide a payout. When a backend constant changes, the matching mirror and its `*RulesTests` change in the same release.

Seeded games share vector files with the backend: `src/Aetherphone.Tests/Vectors/` holds `holdem.json`, `race.json`, `plinko.json`, `originals.json`, `slots-bird.json`, `slots-cascade.json`, `slots-moogle.json`, `blackjack-sidebets.json` and `venue.json`, each a byte-for-byte copy of the file in the backend's test project. Every vector test first pins the file's SHA-256, so a drifted copy fails loudly, then replays each case through the client mirror (`HoldemVectorTests`, `RaceVectors`, `PlinkoVectorTests`, `OriginalsVectorTests`, `SlotsVectorTests`, `BlackjackSideBetVectorTests`, `CasinoVenueVectorTests`). Never edit a vector by hand; copy the backend's file again.

`CasinoVerifier.Verify` checks a settled round from `GET /casino/rounds/{roundId}/verify`: the revealed seed must hash to the commit, then the draw log must replay purpose by purpose (`segment`, `card`, `ball`, `shuffle`, `prize`, `patrons`, `jackpot`, `gamble`, `peg`, `field`, `strength`, `runner`, and the originals' float stream with `mine`, `roll`, `limbo`, `keno`, `card`). Hold'em hands and blackjack hands verify through the room's hand index (`GET /casino/rooms/{roomId}/verify/{index}`), and venue rolls and raffle draws through `VenueVerifier` over `VenueDraws`. Verification fails closed: anything it cannot replay is a mismatch, never a pass.

## Tables, hosting, practice and gil

House tables are furniture the server keeps open: four blackjack tables (`blackjack-pit`, `blackjack-parlour`, `blackjack-salon`, `blackjack-vault`) and the Hold'em rooms (`holdem-low`, `holdem-mid`, `holdem-high`, and `holdem-royal` for the Whale title). The four blackjack tables all take the same bets, from the table minimum up to the player's own ceiling, so they are capacity rather than stake levels: every list shows them as one Blackjack row (`HouseBlackjack.Of` sums the floor and picks the busiest table with a seat left), and tapping it opens the player's own house seat or quick seats them at the busiest table. Hold'em rooms do differ by stakes, so each is listed and named by its blinds. Sitting at a chip table moves a rack from the bankroll chip-to-chip; leaving walks it home. One card-table seat per identity across blackjack and Hold'em.

`HostSheet` is a guided flow that fills a `HostDraft` and builds the `CasinoTableConfig` for `POST /casino/tables`: pick the game from poster tiles (`HostGames`: Blackjack, Hold'em, Dice table, Deathroll, Raffle, and Book while the server lists `venue.book`), pick what it plays for from three cards, set only that game's essentials (stakes on a `LadderSlider` over `HostLadders`, seats as tappable dots, for blackjack who deals, who can join as three icon cards), and open everything else (name, clock, time bank, spectators, practice stack, rebuys, face up, max payout, house rules, ante, dice rounds, venue pin) behind More options. Who deals offers the dealer seat on practice and gil tables, with an auto deal switch that starts off: off, every hand waits for the host's or a co-dealer's Deal and players see "Bets are open. {0} deals when ready."; on, the betting clock deals as at a house table. On a chip table it reads "The clock" with the reason, because the server refuses host dealing, co-dealers and pause there (`practice_only`). A Book (`HostGame.Book`, wire kind `casino.book`) is gil only, so the sheet skips the Play for step (`HostFlowLayout.Steps` renumbers the headers) and asks only for a bank, who can join and the More options that apply (name, max payout, venue pin); `HostDraft.BuildBook` sends seats 0, no dealer, no spectators and no rules, and `CasinoBookRules.Check` mirrors the server's check (`gil_only`, `config_invalid`). A sticky footer summarises the table above Open table. `HostFlowLayout` holds the geometry and is pinned by layout tests. The sheet opens from the venue context card on the Floor and the Venues tab (Host, or Host again on the remembered draft), from the host posters of the Floor's venue section (`HostSheet.EnterGame`: the game preselected, and gil picked when the game takes gil and gil tables are open), from the Blackjack and Hold'em pits and from the Live tab's empty state. Opening a table successfully remembers the draft for that character (`CasinoPreferences.RememberHostDraft` into `Configuration.CasinoHostDrafts`, a `CasinoHostDraftMemory` of plain values and ladder indexes with a `Version` that discards the draft when the ladders change), and Host again restores it (`HostSheet.EnterDraft`) with More options open and the sheet settled on the summary footer and Open table; a remembered Book draft stays out of sight while the server does not list `venue.book` (`HostDraft.Reopens`).

Hosted tables and venue rooms carry a six-letter room code (`CasinoRoomCodes` mirrors the server's alphabet and normalisation; `joinCode` is only sent to members). `RoomCodeCard` shows it in letter cells with Copy on the door and in the table info sheet, and the Venues tab joins with `POST /casino/tables/join` (`code_unknown` when no open table uses it; an old `[aep.casino.v1:...]` token pasted into the same field still resolves). `TableBrowser` (the Venues tab) draws, in this order, the shared venue context card, Join with a code, the My gil ledger card, At this venue (unless the context card is already showing those rows), then the filter rail and the rows: the house floor (one Blackjack row and the Hold'em rooms), mine, invited and listed tables as room cards (`TableRow` over `TableCardLayout`; a Book row reads "Book, {n} entries tonight" once its ledger is known or the host's name until then, opens the door for its host and the player ledger for anyone else, where a patron the Book has not admitted gets the knocker's `TableDoorCard` with Ask to join instead of a ledger that never loads, and never joins the Live board); hosting goes through the context card, so the tab has no Host a table card of its own; `TableDoor` is the host panel (the code card, large Pause, Deal, Co-dealers, Rename and Close buttons, a note on chip tables saying why the first three are missing, knock cards, seated players with Remove, read "Leaving after this hand" while a removed seat plays out its cards, an In the room section listing who is attached without a seat and the co-dealers who are away, each with Remove or the co-dealer switch and never on the host's own row, no seat section in a venue room, no Co-dealers on a poker door with a note saying why, the tournament card; a Book door keeps the code card, Rename, Close, the knocks, the In the room section and the ledger, under a note that the Book runs no game and closes on its own after eight idle hours); `TableLedger` shows the session ledger as a table of players by buy-ins, stack and net and copies it as plain text (a Book has no player table, only entries), and on a gil table the gil entries as `LedgerEntryCards` with I paid, I received and the payee's dispute; a guest entry (`guest`, recorded by the host for a patron without the plugin) carries a Guest tag, offers no button, is listed under its name with "(guest)" in the copied text, and the gil section ends with the night's totals (`GilLedgerTotals`, gil in and gil out over every settled entry the reader is a side of, guests included); while the server lists `venue.book` the host's Record gil section offers a Guest choice beside the seated and registered players (the only choice at a Book, which seats nobody) with a character name field, normalised and bounded by `GuestLedger.Typed` and sent through `CasinoTablesStore.ProposeGuestEntry` with `source: "manual"`; each answer comes back as a toast. `GilLedgerView` (`CasinoScreen.GilLedger`, "My gil ledger") draws the same cards over `GET /casino/ledger`, every entry the player is a side of from the last 30 days at open or closed tables, so a cash-out, raffle ticket or deathroll stake stays confirmable after the table is gone; it opens from a card on the Venues tab, a button under the closed or removed card of a gil table or venue room, and a row in the table sheet of every gil table. The door reads itself every five seconds while drawn; when the table it shows is no longer the reader's, the table card is read again and a 404 pops every route on that table with a closed toast (the server ends a private table nobody has attached to, sat at or watched the door of for fifteen minutes).

At a host-dealt blackjack table (`BlackjackHosting.HostDeals`) the owner deals rather than plays. The server refuses them a new seat (`dealer_cannot_sit`), the empty seats on the rail stop offering Sit, and `BlackjackDeckFlow` gives them the dealer's deck instead of Sit down: Deal as the one primary once a bet is down, held disabled under "Waiting for the first bet" before that, and a short caption while the hand plays out, once it is over and while the table is paused (`BlackjackHosting.DealerDeckOf`). The row above the felt keeps Pause and Ledger and drops its own Deal for them, so Deal appears once. Co-dealers are seated players who were promoted: they keep their seat and their player deck, and Deal stays in the row above the felt for them. Deal on the door deals and opens the table, so the host lands where the hand plays out.

Pause holds a practice or gil table where it stands, and the server enforces it: a blackjack table refuses bets, decisions and Deal with `table_paused`, deals nothing, runs no turn or insurance clock and auto-stands nobody until Resume, which restarts the window that was running (a whole turn for the seat on its turn). The room carries `paused` with no deadline, so every seated deck shows "The host paused the table" with only Stand (plus Rebuy for a broke seat that may rebuy, while one that cannot gets the Broke deck described with tournaments below), the state line reads Paused, the dealer's deck reads the paused caption, and the door holds Deal. A venue room fires no due timer while paused, refuses acts with `table_paused`, carries `paused` (and its current name) in its room state so the ribbon reads "The host paused the table" and the controls hold, and moves each deadline on by the pause when it resumes; a Hold'em table finishes the hand in play, deals no next one, holds its sit-out clock, and its ribbon reads the pause between hands.

The blackjack felt stacks the dealer, the state line, the seat rail and your own hands in fitted zones (`BlackjackTableLayout`), and `BlackjackTableLayout.MinimumHeight` is the shortest felt they fit in without touching, every gap and card at its floor. Everything around the felt yields to that floor: the hosted row keeps the bank and house rules as wrapped pills only while they fit, merges them into one scrolling line beside its buttons or on a line of its own when they do not (`HostedInfoLayout.Mode`), squeezes Pause, Deal and Ledger to share a narrow row, and a host's knock card or a refusal notice lays over the felt (`FeltTop`, `FeltBottom`) instead of squeezing it under its floor. Rail plates fall back to the forename when a full name does not fit and show gil in compact amounts.

Knocks follow through on both sides. The host's blackjack, Hold'em and room views read the door on the same cadence and show the first waiting knock above the felt (`HostKnockBanner` over the door's own `KnockCard`). The knocker's closed card (`TableDoorCard`) runs off a `CasinoKnockStatus` in `CasinoTablesStore` (`CasinoKnocks` maps a refused attach, a knock answer and a card read to None, Waiting, Admitted, Denied or Refused): while waiting it reads the table card every three seconds, re-attaches on its own once admitted, says "Request declined" with Ask again when turned away, and titles a kicked or banned answer "Removed from this table". A waiting knock survives leaving the table and is read every fifteen seconds in the background; `CasinoKnockNotifier` tells the knocker when they are let in or turned away, and tells a host who is not on their door or table about each new knock (it polls `GET /casino/tables/{id}/knocks` every twenty seconds for the table the host owns). Joining a Knock table by code or opening a pasted invite at the door opens the table on that card.

Spectators off stops watching without a seat, never joining. An Open table with spectators off still lets anyone in while a seat is free, so the seat is picked and bought into on the felt as usual; the server shows a visitor out with `no_spectators` after two minutes without a seat and turns watchers away once the table is full. Knock and Private tables keep watching to the seated, the co-dealers and the allowlist, and venue rooms ignore the switch because everyone in them is playing.

The table's currency (`CasinoCurrencies`) decides how money moves:

- **Chips** (blackjack and Hold'em): the bank, every economy rule, standard rules only.
- **Practice** (blackjack and Hold'em): the table never touches the bank. No sitting, rack, round, meter, XP, mission or jackpot; every seat starts at the practice stack and may rebuy when the host allows; with rebuys off, a player who stands and sits again keeps the stack they left with and is refused (`rebuy_off`) when that is below the minimum bet or the big blind. The stage shows grey chips and the practice ribbon, the cashier never opens, and hands are recorded only in the local History group. Fairness is unchanged: hands still draw from the room's seed chain and verify.
- **Gil** (blackjack and venue rooms): host-banked. The host declares a bank, a max bet and a max payout per hand, and a bet whose worst case the bank cannot cover is refused (`bank_limit`). The server never holds or converts gil; it keeps a two-sided ledger in which a buy-in, rebuy or payout settles only when both sides confirm it, unconfirmed entries show amber on both screens, and a payee can dispute a payout that never arrived. Gil tables touch no coin, chip, XP, mission, club point or Hall of Fame row. Gil tables take no side bets and offer neither insurance nor surrender. Most patrons at a venue have no plugin, so the host can also book a guest: `CasinoTablesStore.ProposeGuestEntry` sends `POST /casino/tables/{id}/ledger` with a `guestName` and no counterparty, and the server answers a one-sided entry that is settled at once, counts in no reputation and cannot be disputed (`guest_entry`). A Book (`casino.book`, `CasinoWire.BookKind`) is a gil ledger with trade sync and no game, for venues that run games the app does not have: a hosted table with a bank, an optional max payout, a listing and a location, no seats and no room socket, exempt from the empty-table sweep and ended by the server eight idle hours after its last door read or entry (`CasinoHostingRules.BookIdleHours`). Both ride on the `venue.book` feature flag (`CasinoFeatures.VenueBook`); an older server shows no guest option, no Book poster and no Book in the host sheet.

`CasinoCurrencies.SeatBanked(currency)` is true for practice and gil: the stage balance is the seat's own stack, not the bankroll.

## Venue layer

Hosted venue rooms (`casino.dice-table`, `casino.deathroll`, `casino.raffle`) run on practice chips or gil, open from the table browser, the door or a deep link into `CasinoScreen.VenueRoom`, and are drawn by `Venue/VenueCabinet.cs` on the Strip with a room ribbon. Each room has a Dalamud-free playback (`DiceTablePlayback`, `DeathrollPlayback`, `RafflePlayback`) that takes the room's GameState and choreographs only what the server returned; a mid-event join snaps to the current state. `CasinoVenueStore` parses the GameState off the draw path (`VenueRoomView`), sends `POST /casino/venue/{roomId}/act` with an idempotent `clientActionId`, verifies the last roll or draw through `GET /casino/rooms/{roomId}/verify/{seq}` (`VenueVerifier` over `VenueDraws`, pinned by `venue.json`), polls `GET /casino/tables/nearby` from the housing position (`IHousingPositionSource`), and indexes listed tables by venue address for the Venues app pill, which sits on the venue cards and on the venue detail hero and opens the table. A dice table running highest-roll rounds takes one roll per player per open round: the server refuses a repeat with `already_rolled`, the roll button holds and reads You rolled this round once your id is in the round's rollers, and the Recent rolls log scrolls in its own child through the 50 rolls the room keeps. Free rolling and rolls between rounds stay unlimited.

The Floor's venue section (`Venue/VenueSection`) is the first browse section, right after the wins ticker: a Venues header, the venue context card and a rail of host posters (Blackjack, Hold'em, Dice table, Deathroll, Raffle, and a sixth, Book, while `venue.book` is open: `VenuePosters.Count`) drawn with the `PosterTile` statics on the shelf geometry. Each poster carries a plays-for line from `VenuePosters.MetaOf` over `HostGames.Accepts` ("Gil, practice or chips", "Chips or practice", "Gil or practice", "Gil or free tickets", "Gil, any game" for the Book, or the practice-only lines when gil tables are closed) and a Gil badge while `gil.tables` is open; a tap opens the host sheet on that game with gil preselected when it takes gil. `VenueContextCard` (one instance for the Floor, one for the Venues tab) picks a state through `VenueSectionState.Pick`, in this order: Your table when the account owns an open hosted table or venue room (`CasinoTablesStore.HostedTableId`: the game tile, the table name (`TableBrowser.NameOf`: the server stores no default name, so an untitled table or Book reads "{0}'s table" or "{0}'s book" after its host in the reader's language), the seats or room count, the bank and max payout on a gil table or the stakes line otherwise, the six-letter room code in compact letter cells, Door and Ledger buttons or a single Open the ledger for a Book, and the card body opens the table; the lines wrap and the card grows to fit), At this venue when `NearbyTablesCard` has rows (its own title and rows), Host again when a draft is remembered for the character (the `HostDraftSummary` line, Host and New table), else Host a table with one Host button. `VenueContextLayout` holds the card geometry and is pinned by layout tests.

The stage Extra button on blackjack tables and venue rooms opens `VenueTableSheet`: verify, trade sync settings (gil tables), the broadcast view for spectators and the report action (`Plugin.Report`, target `casino_table`). The table's owner also gets Host controls, which opens the door on top of the table. Backing out of a table onto its own door keeps the seat and the room (`CasinoRoute.KeepsSeat`), the door's Open the table goes back to it (`CasinoRoute.ReturnsToTable`), and the seat is only left when the door itself is closed. A practice deathroll shows your stack above its deck, and the newest finished duel keeps its rolls so the losing 1 lands on screen and verifies. Live lists a venue room by everyone in it, since it has no seats.

Trade sync is opt-in (`Configuration.CasinoTradeSync`). `TradeWindowReader` listens to the `Trade` addon lifecycle and reads the partner's name from `TradeStringArray.TradePartnerName` and both offers from `TradeNumberArray` (`SendGilCount`, `ReceiveGilCount`), falling back to the window's text nodes; `TradeSyncTracker` confirms a completed trade from the wallet delta against the wallet the window opened with. `TradeTableMemory` (over `TradeContexts`) picks the gil table the trade belongs to: the open gil blackjack room, the open gil venue room (dice table, deathroll, raffle), the host's door or the open ledger page of a Book (`CasinoTablesStore.LedgerRoomId`, for the host and the patron alike), else the last of those seen in the past four hours by the same account, else the host's own gil table or Book in the directory. A Book is located like a gil venue room but its context is not flagged `Venue`, so a registered buy-in at it proposes freely instead of only toward a larger waiting entry, as the server allows. Gil Hold'em does not exist (the server refuses a gil poker table), so it is never a trade table.

`TradeLedgerMatcher` first compares the window's names with the public names the table shows (seats, venue participants, ledger parties and the host). When that finds nobody, `TradeMatchFlow` sends the window text and the gil to `POST /casino/tables/{id}/ledger/match`, which compares it with the character names the server holds and answers with only a user id, a public name and the caller's side; a 404 from an older server is no match. Either way the result becomes a confirmation of an unsettled entry for that amount and direction, or a buy-in or payout proposal (`source: "trade"`); the server folds any proposal, typed or traded, into the entry the other side already recorded and splits a larger waiting entry when a trade pays only part of it (one trade carries at most a million gil). A venue room proposes a buy-in only toward a larger waiting entry, and a player only proposes to the host. The host always gets a one-tap prompt (`TradeSyncPrompt`, queued in `TradeProposalQueue` so a second match waits behind an unanswered one) plus a notification; only a player sees the switch that lets their own side confirm automatically, and the server's answer comes back as a toast. A host payout to a seated player comes off that sitting's stack when it settles, and a stand books only the stack left after the sitting's waiting payouts. The server is asked about one trade at a time; one more completed trade waits for that answer and is matched after it, and a third becomes a miss. A trade that matched nobody shows as an amber line in that table's gil ledger for ten minutes, worded by its `TradeMissKind`: received from or sent to someone not at this table when the server found nobody, or could not be matched to this table when the server was unreachable, refused, found a registered player no entry can take, read no names or was never asked. Only a host miss with no registered player behind it (no match, no names, or the trade that missed its turn) becomes a guest offer: `GuestLedger.Offer` decides it when the trade misses, and the prompt shows it only while the server lists `venue.book`, checked as it draws. The host gets a notification and a prompt with the partner's character name from the trade arrays and a button that names the direction (Record guest buy-in, Record guest rebuy for a guest already booked, Record guest payout), sent through `CasinoTablesStore.ProposeGuestEntry` with `source: "trade"`; a guest offer for a trade that was never checked says so. Not now keeps the amber line, which carries the same button and prefills the Record gil section instead: the Guest choice, Buy-in or Payout, the amount, and the name when it was read. For thirty seconds after a host miss the trade sync re-reads that table's ledger every five seconds, and an entry for the same amount and direction that now waits only on the host (the patron's own trade sync recorded it) clears the amber line and becomes a Confirm in ledger prompt. A ledger refresh asked for while one is in flight runs again when that one lands. A player never sees the guest offer, and a name that only came from scraped window text never becomes a guest. Every decision is logged under `[TradeSync]` without any names. Nothing in the game is automated.

`BroadcastView` (`CasinoScreen.Broadcast`, landscape) projects the blackjack or Hold'em snapshot into `BroadcastTable` with large seats, stacks and cards and no controls. When the table ends the broadcast clears and says why instead of holding its last frame. `TournamentOverlay` draws the practice blackjack tournament strip, leaderboard, eliminations and winner over the table; the standings open once when the winner is called and close on a tap on the strip or the board, and both claim their area so the felt underneath never takes the tap. Hosts start and stop it from `TournamentDoorCard` on the door, which holds Start until two players are seated, says when the host still has to sit or deals the table and so cannot enter, and checks the starting stack before sending; the server names those refusals `tournament_players` and `tournament_stack`, and coin and gil blackjack doors only note that tournaments are practice-table only. The winner banner wraps (`GameBanner.DrawWrapped`) and no elimination notice is drawn over it. A seat below the minimum bet with nothing bet gets the Broke deck: Stand, the ledger on a gil table, and a line saying it is out of the tournament, that rebuys pause during a tournament, or that the host turned rebuys off. With rebuys off, a practice seat that stands and sits again keeps the stack it left with, and one that left below the minimum bet is refused with `rebuy_off`.

## Lobby

The app has four tabs (`CasinoTab`: Floor, Live, Venues (the `Tables` member, titled `L.VenueHub.Tab`, anchor `casino.tab.tables`), Cashier); every other page is a `CasinoRoute` on the router (`CasinoScreen`: Cabinet, Table, Pit, TableDoor, HostTable, TableLedger, VenueRoom, Broadcast, DailySpin, History, Fairness, Limits, RoundDetail, Fame).

- **Floor** (`CasinoApp.Lobby`, `Strip/`): the chips hero (`StripHero`: the chip glyph and balance with a Get chips capsule beside it, which takes its own row only when the two do not fit, then the coin value with the balance title, the `LevelCapsule`, one claims row holding the timed Claim and a Free spin pill that shows only while the daily spin is ready, split by `SharedClaimSplit` with a label that does not fit scrolling on hover instead of being cut, and no claims row at all when neither is there), the resume card when seated, the missions and club status (`MissionsCapsule` and `ClubCapsule`, both painted by `FloorCapsule`: two half-width tiles in one row, Missions with its done count and the club tier with its progress bar, falling back to stacked full-width capsules when either line would not fit or only one is shown; missions opens `MissionsSheet` hosting `MissionsCard` and its claims), the hero carousel (`StripCarousel`: jackpot marquee, a machine with live idle reels, the next race, a hot table, Dealer Hold'em dealing its idle hand, the live challenge; the page dots only with more than one page), the wins ticker (`WinsTicker`, fed by the `casino-floor` room), the venue section (`Venue/VenueSection`: the Venues header, the venue context card and the host posters, described under the venue layer), the Live right now rail (Race when open, Wheel and Bingo always present, an idle room waiting on the next round without a live dot or crowd line, then the busy tables), the Jump back in rail (up to four recently played shelf games picked by `CasinoRecentGames.Collect` from the history store, hidden without history), the shelves (`StripShelves` over `StripCatalog`: Cards, Machines, Quick bets, with Barkeep wearing a Skill badge), the Hall of Fame podium (`FamePodium`, full boards in `FameView`) and the records row (History, Fairness, Limits). `StripIntro` is the first-visit walkthrough.
- **Live** (`LiveBoard`): rooms, the one house Blackjack row, the Hold'em rooms and listed tables with phase, occupancy and Watch, filtered by All, Open seats, Practice, Friends and High roller. The app refreshes the Hold'em directory alongside the blackjack one, so Hold'em rooms show without visiting the Hold'em pit first.
- **Venues** (`TableBrowser`): the venue context card (Your table, At this venue or Host again), Join with a code, My gil ledger, At this venue, then the browser with its filter rail and quick seat; the pushed `CasinoScreen.Tables` route carries the same title.
- **Cashier** (`CashierPanel`, shared by the tab and `CashierDrawer`): both balances (wallet coins, bankroll chips and their coin value, the rate), Buy chips (`CashierBuyIn`: any whole number of coins up to the wallet, three one-tap amounts sized to the wallet by `ChipsAmounts.Quick`) and Cash out (`CashierCashOut`: one tap converts every chip, with a check only while a round is still in play). The chips capsule on every stage opens the same drawer and prints the stack's coin value beside it.

Missions, challenges, the Hall of Fame and the feed only count real-chip rounds. Hall of Fame boards only name players who opted in through the existing leaderboard flag.

## Adding a game

The server ships the game first: its kind, routes, feature flag, and a vector file when the game is seeded. Then, on the client:

1. **Ids.** Add the game id to `Apps/Casino/CasinoGames.cs` and the wire kind to `Core/Casino/CasinoWire.cs`. Add the flag to `CasinoFeatures`, `CasinoFeatureSet.Known` and `CasinoGameGate.FlagFor`.
2. **Wire.** Add the DTOs to a `Casino*Dtos.cs` file (every field defaulted) and register them in `AethernetJsonContext`; add the routes to `CasinoClient` (a new partial file for a new area); pin route and JSON shape in a `*WireContractTests` class. New refusal reasons go into `CasinoReasons` with a string and a `CasinoReasonCoverageTests` entry.
3. **Rules and vectors.** Mirror the pay tables and draws in `Core/Casino/<Game>Rules.cs` with tests against the backend constants. Copy the backend vector file verbatim into `src/Aetherphone.Tests/Vectors/`, pin its SHA-256 and replay it. Teach `CasinoVerifier` the game's draw purposes.
4. **Store.** Extend `CasinoPlayStore` for a solo game or `CasinoRoomsStore` for a room; results come back through `Take*`, never through a callback into Draw.
5. **Cabinet.** A class under its own `Apps/Casino/<Game>/` folder with `Draw(stage, frame, ui)`, a `CasinoStageSpec`, a playback class, `BetComposer` for every stake, `stage.Celebration` for every win, `stage.Settle` for every settled round, and `ICabinetIdle`. Follow the full screen and legibility rules above.
6. **Host wiring.** In `CasinoApp`: the cabinet field, its case in `DrawStage`, its spec in `StageSpecFor`, its branch in `ResetCabinetOf` (check after merges that no branch ends in a doubled `return;`), and `StageBalance` if it shows a stack other than the bankroll.
7. **Lobby.** A `StripCatalog` entry on the right shelf (Cards, Machines or Quick bets; a clocked room goes on `CasinoApp.LiveRooms` and the live rail instead of a shelf), a `CasinoSign` word, the name in `CasinoGameNames`, the rules sheet steps and pitch in `CasinoRules`, a glyph in `CasinoGlyphs`, an accent in `CasinoArt`, the minimum bet and printed return in `CasinoApp.Floor`, and the mission sentence in `MissionText`. A shelf game joins the Jump back in rail by itself once it has been played; `CasinoFloorLayoutTests` pins that every playable id is reached exactly once.
8. **Copy.** Every string as a `LocString` in its own nested class in `L.cs` plus all nine JSONs. Game names never take an "Aether" prefix.

## Other pieces

- `PhaseRibbon.Draw(drawList, rect, label, remainingMs, windowSeconds, crowd, accent, scale)`: the room ribbon with a `TurnTimerRing`, the only countdown in the app.
- `CasinoNotice.Draw` and `DrawWithAction`: the Reason, Info and Card notice shapes (Card bodies at Subheadline, the rest at Footnote).
- `ChipStack` (Windows/Components/Layout): ten denominations from 100 to 100M with fixed colours, five discs a column, notched edges, a practice variant, and compact amounts.
- `NumberText.Compact` reads K, M, B and T without ever rounding a balance up; `NumberText.Signed` caches the plus form; `CasinoTextCache` caches every other label a cabinet formats.
- `DailySpinWidget` (Apps/Casino/Widgets) puts the turning idle wheel on the home screen.
