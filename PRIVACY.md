# Aetherphone Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2 October 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use the Aetherphone plugin, the Aetherphone companion app, and Aethernet, the online service behind their social features. Features of the plugin that work offline, such as wallpapers, timers and notes, keep their data on your computer.

## Who we are and how to contact us

Aethernet is operated by the maintainer of the Aetherphone project, publicly known as XeldarAlz, who is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. We are a non-commercial volunteer project, not a registered company.

For any privacy question or request, write to **privacy@aetherphone.net**. A postal address for formal notices is available on request.

## What we collect

**Your character and account.** You sign in by proving that you control a FINAL FANTASY XIV character, through the Lodestone, XIVAuth, or Rising Stones (石之家) on the Chinese client. We store the character's name, home world and region, and which Lodestone profile or Rising Stones account it belongs to. We never ask for your game password, an email address or your date of birth. Each character you verify has its own account.

**Your profile.** Your display name, a handle and in-app phone number that we create at random, your bio, pictures and settings, and any profiles you create in individual apps.

**What you post and share.** Posts, comments, photos, videos, voice notes, stories, reactions, poll votes, listings, events, radio stations, game rooms, and anything you send through the Feedback app. We also keep who you follow, your contacts and who you block. Kupo confessions are shown to others without your name, but we store them with your account.

**Private messages.** Your ChocoChat, Velvet, Aethergram and Yellow Pages conversations, with their photos and voice notes. Whether we can read them is explained under Encrypted messages.

**Velvet.** If you use Velvet: your Velvet profile, posts, connections and messages, when you confirmed that you are 18 or older, and your character's race, which on the global client we confirm from its public Lodestone profile.

**Activity.** When you were last active, when you read a conversation, and your time zone; you can hide these from other players in your settings. Which posts your feeds showed you and which stories you viewed. Your calls: who took part, when and for how long. Your Aether Coin and Gamba history, used to run games and detect cheating.

**Reports and moderation.** Reports you make and reports about you, notes moderators write, notices we send you, and any action taken on your account or content, including suspensions.

**Patreon.** If you link Patreon: which Patreon account you linked, whether your membership is active, and your pledge amount and tier. We never receive your payment details.

**Devices and connections.** The version of the app you use and where you installed it from, so that we can support current versions and refuse unofficial ones. For the companion app, the address our notifications are sent to, and your phone's type, language and name. Your IP address reaches us with every request. We use it only to keep the service safe and do not store it, except in a record we keep when you link or unlink Patreon. The providers that carry our traffic also see it.

**On your computer.** The plugin stores your encryption key, a copy of the messages it has unlocked for you, and an archive of your in-game tells (which you can turn off) on your PC.

We do not show advertising, use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services, or sell personal data.

## Encrypted messages

ChocoChat, Velvet, Aethergram and Yellow Pages conversations are end-to-end encrypted, with an encryption key the app creates for you automatically. Messages, photos and voice notes are locked on the sender's device so that only the people in the conversation can read them, and we store them locked. Your key belongs to your account, so every device you link can read your conversations.

We keep a locked backup of your encryption key on our server. Only your recovery code can open it, and we never receive that code.

Even in a locked conversation, we can see details such as who is in it, who sent each message and when, the type and size of each message, and whether it was read. Group chat names, pictures and descriptions are not locked.

Some text is unlocked by design:

- **Reports:** a message you report from the plugin reaches moderators unlocked, with up to five earlier messages from the conversation and their photos and voice notes.
- **Translation:** the text of a message you translate is sent to us unlocked.
- **Velvet requests:** the introduction sent with a connection request is not locked, so that harassment can be reported.

If a member of a conversation has no encryption key, which can happen on some computers, the app marks the conversation **Not encrypted** with an open-lock icon, and its messages are stored on our server in readable form. A locked conversation can also contain older messages that were stored readable before the current safeguards existed.

Calls are off until you turn them on. Call audio is protected while it travels and passes through our server. Calls are not end-to-end encrypted and are never recorded.

## Translation

When you tap **Translate**, or turn on translation for a chat or for your feeds, Aethernet translates the text using a translation service provider. Only the text you choose to translate is sent, never your account details. The provider processes it only on our behalf, does not use it to improve its products, and keeps it only for a limited time to detect abuse and meet legal requirements.

We never store translations of private messages. We keep translations of public content for reuse, without recording who asked. We count how many items you translate each day to apply a daily limit.

## How we use your data and why

| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Providing your account and the features you use, including the translations you ask for | Performing our agreement with you (the Terms of Service) |
| Verifying your character, and refusing suspended characters and unofficial versions of the app | Legitimate interests |
| Keeping the service safe: reports, moderation, preventing abuse and cheating, and Velvet's age and race rules | Legitimate interests, and legal obligation where one applies |
| Fixing faults, keeping the service running and making backups | Legitimate interests |
| Optional features, such as linking Patreon or sharing your in-game location in a chat | Consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Velvet profile details that can reveal your sex life or sexual orientation | Your choice to add them and show them to other Velvet users |
| Answering legal requests | Legal obligation |

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have weighed them against your rights, and you can object at any time.

**Moderation.** Moderation is done by people and starts from reports. Moderators may also review public content. No AI or automated system screens your content, and we do not use your content to train AI.

**Automatic decisions.** Some rules apply automatically: a suspended character cannot sign in, a blocked version of the app is refused, and Velvet is refused to Lalafell characters and, on the global client, to characters whose race we have not confirmed. Suspensions and blocks are decided by people. You can contest any of these decisions by writing to us.

## Sensitive information

Some Velvet profile details, such as gender, sexuality, relationship status, kinks and limits, can reveal information about your sex life or sexual orientation. They are optional. You decide whether to add them, they are shown to other Velvet users who view your profile, and you can change or remove them at any time.

## Who receives your data

- **Other players** see what you post and share, and the people you message receive your messages. Other players do not see your character name and home world, except in Muster, where an event's host sees those of everyone who RSVPs and players who are going see the host's, and in ChocoChat chat titles and companion app notifications when you have not set a display name. If you share a MogCast stream or let people nearby find your Jam, players in your zone and world can find and join it.
- **Moderators** see the data they need to review reports and enforce the rules, including your character name and home world. Access to private conversations is limited and logged, and locked messages stay unreadable to moderators.
- **Our service providers:** a cloud hosting provider that runs the service; a network and storage provider that carries traffic to the service and stores media and backups; a translation service provider; and providers that host our community radio server, run our backups and deliver our internal alerts.
- **Notification services** built into iOS and Android deliver companion app notifications. A notification includes the sender's name and, for comments and unlocked ChocoChat messages, their text.
- **Sign-in services:** the Lodestone, XIVAuth and Rising Stones confirm that you control your character.
- **Patreon**, if you link it, is an independent service with its own privacy policy.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it or where it is necessary to investigate a serious safety issue. We will tell you when we are allowed to.

Some apps in the plugin fetch information directly from other services, from your computer, for example market prices, venue and event listings, music and video, character portraits and game data. These services receive your IP address and what you asked for, which can include character names or your Lodestone profile, and they have their own privacy policies. A login you enter for another service inside an app, such as Faloop in the Hunts app, goes directly to that service.

## International transfers

Our providers may process your data in countries other than your own. When data leaves the UK or the European Economic Area, we rely on standard contractual clauses, the data protection terms approved for this purpose, which our providers include in their agreements. Signing in through Rising Stones involves a check with Rising Stones in China. Our translation provider may process the text you translate in any country where it operates.

## How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy.

| Data | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Account, profile and settings | Until you delete your account |
| Posts, comments and other content | Until you delete it or your account |
| Private messages | Until you delete them or your account, or 7 days after everyone in the conversation has cleared it. A Velvet conversation is deleted 7 days after the connection ends |
| Stories, listings, events, notifications and game rooms | Deleted automatically within 60 days after they end |
| Which posts your feeds showed you | 4 days |
| Call records and Aether Coin history | Until you delete your account |
| Gamba records | Up to 180 days for most records |
| Reports and moderation records | As long as needed for safety and abuse prevention, including after your account is deleted. The text and media of removed content are deleted 7 days after removal |
| Records linked to your character, such as a suspension | Up to 365 days after your account is deleted |
| Backups | Deleted data leaves our backups after about 30 days |

Copies made during maintenance or recovery work can be kept outside these periods.

When you delete your account, your profile and content are removed immediately, and the data listed above is kept only for the periods shown. The [account deletion page](https://aetherphone.net/delete-account/) explains what is deleted and what is kept.

## Your rights and choices

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it deleted, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable form where this applies, and withdraw consent at any time. We apply these rights to every user, wherever you live.

To use any of these rights, write to **privacy@aetherphone.net**. We respond within one month.

The fastest way to delete your account is in the plugin: open **Settings**, then **Account**, then **Delete account**. Deletion takes effect immediately. It is not available while the account is suspended. In the companion app, open **Settings**, then **Delete account**. You can also ask us by email, and the [account deletion page](https://aetherphone.net/delete-account/) explains every option.

In your settings you can make your account private and choose who can mention or tag you.

You can also complain to your data protection authority, such as the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Children

Aethernet is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. We do not ask for or check your age. Velvet is only for adults aged 18 and over: you confirm your age with one tap before entering, and we record when you did, but we do not verify it.

If you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us at **privacy@aetherphone.net** and we will remove it. Child safety concerns can also be sent to **childsafety@aetherphone.net**.

## Security

Connections between the apps and Aethernet are encrypted, and access to our servers is limited to the maintainers who need it. No service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

## Changes to this policy

We will announce material changes in the plugin and update the date at the top of this page.
