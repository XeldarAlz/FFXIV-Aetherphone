# Aetherphone Trademark and Naming Policy

**Last updated:** 2 October 2026

The Aetherphone client is free software under the AGPL-3.0-or-later ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)), and you are free to fork it, change it, and distribute your version. That licence covers the code. It does not cover the name.

"Aetherphone" and "Aethernet", the phone logo and plugin icon, the app icons, and the visual identity built from them are the project's marks. They tell a player which build they are installing and who stands behind it, and they are not licensed with the source.

## Forks do not use the official servers

Aethernet is a service we host, and it is no more part of the licence than the name is. A fork, a patched or recompiled build, a rehosted copy, or a client written against our endpoints does not get to connect to it, whatever was changed and whatever the fork is called. Run your own back end for your own version: the AGPL leaves you free to do exactly that.

Section 11 of the [Terms of Service](TERMS.md) is the binding rule, not this page. Unofficial versions can be warned or blocked from connecting to Aethernet.

## If you fork Aetherphone

Give your version its own name and its own icon before you publish it.

- Do not call it Aetherphone, and do not use a name that would be mistaken for it, including "Aetherphone" with a word attached on either side
- Do not ship the plugin icon or the app icons as your own identity
- Do not call your service Aethernet
- Do not publish it in a Dalamud plugin repository in a way that suggests it is our build, an official version, or a continuation of this project
- Do not connect it to the official Aethernet servers, as above
- Say plainly what changed, so nobody reports your bugs to us

## What you can do without asking

You can name us. Saying your project is "based on Aetherphone" or "a fork of Aetherphone", linking to this repository, and keeping the copyright and licence notices the AGPL already requires are all fine, and the last of those is mandatory. Writing about Aetherphone, reviewing it, streaming it, or discussing it needs no permission at all.

Contributed artwork stays with the artists who made it, credited where it appears in the plugin. Their work is not yours to reuse either, and the credit does not transfer with a fork.

## What needs permission

Using the name or the marks for your own build, service, repository, community, or anything sold, or in any way that implies we endorse or run what you are offering.

## Why

Players install a plugin by name from a repository they trust. A fork wearing this one's name inherits its reputation and sends its support and moderation load to us, for code we did not write and cannot fix. Renaming costs you an hour and settles all of it.
