Aetherphone libretro core manifest
=================================

Aetherphone does not distribute any libretro module. Syrcus downloads
the module for a system from the official libretro buildbot the first time you
open that system, and only after you confirm the download:

  https://buildbot.libretro.com/nightly/windows/x86_64/latest/

Each archive is verified against the CRC-32 the buildbot publishes in that
directory's .index-extended manifest before it is installed. Modules are kept
in the plugin's config folder and loaded at runtime through the public libretro
ABI. They remain separate works under their own licenses; they are not
relicensed as part of Aetherphone's AGPL-3.0 code.
