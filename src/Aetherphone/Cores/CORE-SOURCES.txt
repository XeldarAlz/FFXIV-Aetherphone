Aetherphone libretro core manifest
=================================

The files below are unmodified libretro modules loaded at runtime through the
public libretro ABI. They remain separate works under their own licenses; they
are not relicensed as part of Aetherphone's AGPL-3.0 code. The plugin and the
modules are distributed together as an aggregate.

The source revision and SHA-256 recorded for each binary identify the exact
source snapshot and artifact included in this distribution. Corresponding
source is available from each permanent commit URL.
